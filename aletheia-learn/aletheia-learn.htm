Accessibility without removing the learning

Ask for READ ALOUD, SPEAK/DICTATE, SIMPLIFY, DEFINE, VISUAL, TRANSLATE, FOCUS, SLOW/CHUNK or another suitable response format. The aim is to remove irrelevant access friction while keeping the useful thinking.
