What you'll learn

An original Aletheia course broadly following Harvard's archived AI teaching sequence: understanding, using and evaluating AI.

01 · Understand

What AI and language models do, and why believable answers can be wrong.

02 · Prepare

Set preferences, instructions, project context, privacy and honesty rules.

03 · Ask naturally

Conversational prompting, optional TOCC, Ask → Walkabout → Check → Answer.

04 · Use tools

Retrieval, project knowledge and deciding when AI is the right tool.

05 · Check claims

Misinformation, synthetic media, sources, uncertainty, risk and permissions.

06 · Build

Make one real AI workflow and test it for evidence of genuine learning.
