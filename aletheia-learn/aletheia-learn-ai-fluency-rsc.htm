Borrow before you buy

Try your local library, an ebook or an audiobook. AI changes quickly, so books are strongest for lasting principles, not current feature lists.

Useful learning gifts

Choose by practical need rather than buying expensive gadgets labelled “AI”.

Comfortable headset with microphone

Useful for voice conversations with an AI tutor and listening to spoken lessons. Look for comfortable fit and a clear microphone.

Adjustable desk stand

Keeps course instructions visible while typing or speaking. Prioritise stability and adjustable viewing angle.

Optional

Bluetooth keyboard

Helpful when practising longer prompts on a tablet or phone. Check compatibility and whether the learner would rather use voice.
