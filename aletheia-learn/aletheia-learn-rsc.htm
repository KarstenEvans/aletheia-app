Cheap additions that matter

Folding stand, long USB-C lead, power bank, decent headset/microphone, paper notebook or a little whiteboard. None is glamorous; all can make practice easier.

Keyboard ≠ magic memory device. A keyboard's value is input efficiency and making substantial active answers practical. Retrieval and generation are the learning mechanisms. Handwriting can also be useful.

Shopping caution: avoid anonymous mini-projectors based only on huge claimed lumen/resolution numbers. Check native resolution, a meaningful brightness measurement, throw distance, casting/input support, fan noise, returns and independent reviews.

Science Museum AiRo AI Robot

Hands-on AI/robotics learning toy: gestures, voice commands and Bluetooth control. Ages 8+, listed at £70 on the official UK shop.

Sphero Mini programmable robot

App-controlled programmable robot ball, a coding and logic learning gift. Check current UK stock before purchasing.

Solar Einstein desk figure

Light-powered science desk mascot; a playful nod to curiosity, not an AI device. Verify UK delivery costs.

AI-labelled toys range from actual programming/voice-recognition kits to simple remote-control devices; read the manufacturer's specifications instead of assuming every toy contains a generative AI model.
