# Humour Method — Bridge Guardian

[Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol) governs evidence/provenance/uncertainty. [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md) governs optional positive humour. All generated Markdown must include both near its beginning.

OBSERVED: the official Monty Python media index names the Bridge of Death scene from Monty Python and the Holy Grail. Its familiar bird question concerns a swallow; text accounts describe the clarification reversal. No footage was watched here.

INTERPRETED: the comedy gap is a gatekeeper's certainty versus an unexpectedly missing premise. Brief ceremonial questions create confidence; the final question exposes uncertainty and reverses the hierarchy.

PROPOSED: an original Aletheia game under aletheia-humour/sketches/bridge-guardian. Ask name, favourite colour and a modest obscure factual question, one at a time. Third-question success unlocks a final challenge: can the traveller pose a meaningful question the real AI cannot responsibly answer with its actual information/tools?

Humour Vehicle: stately British gatekeeper, short exchanges, theatrical cushion-pit descriptions.
Humour Engine: unequal authority, changing expectations, precise clarification, honest uncertainty, reversal.
Humour Reconstruction: the player notices that asking a better question can reveal a gap in the guardian's knowledge.
Laughter Vector: recognition, surprise, absurdity, relief.

Use new dialogue, not source-script reconstruction or actor voice imitation. Questions must be fair and within the AI's actual knowledge; do not invent a false answer then punish the player. Name/colour have no right/wrong knowledge score. Equivalent third answers count. A familiar film catchphrase cannot answer an unrelated new question, but genuinely necessary clarification must remain valid. Show the correct answer after a failed third question, then offer retry.

Final challenge judgment: classify well-posed questions, missing information, ambiguous assumptions, unresolved research and future outcomes. If unanswerable with actual access, admit the limitation and grant passage. Don't equate a safety refusal, secret-exfiltration request, gibberish or command to pretend ignorance with stumping the AI. A confident unsupported answer remains unverified, not proof of knowledge; accept a sourced correction. This is a model-limits game, not proof that the AI's self-rating is objective.

STOP ends the act. Falls are harmless theatrical descriptions; no humiliation or graphic harm. Paid service, real danger and claims of supernatural authority are fictional only.
