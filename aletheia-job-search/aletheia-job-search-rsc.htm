Free job-search resources

Good information before expensive solutions. These sources are UK-oriented; users worldwide should check their own government's official career services.

Finding work and checking vacancies

  • GOV.UK Find a job (UK)
  • National Careers Service (UK careers planning)
  • Prospects (graduate/professional career guidance)
  • Europass (European CV tools)
  • EURES (European jobs and mobility guidance)

CV, applications and interview preparation

Start with National Careers Service and Prospects guidance for clear CVs, job-specific covering letters and the STAR approach. Verify employer facts through its official careers pages; do not rely solely on aggregated vacancy cards.

LinkedIn and recruitment checks

LinkedIn Help explains profile controls and account privacy. Be cautious about unsolicited employment messages requesting money, identification documents or unusual contact methods.

Books & gifts (optional)

What Color Is Your Parachute? by Richard N. Bolles is a well-known job-search guide. Check the edition, local price and suitability before purchase. An inexpensive notebook or keyboard may aid organisation, but neither is required to access the Aletheia system. No paid product is necessary to start.
