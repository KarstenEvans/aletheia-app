What a binaural beat is

Two steady tones with slightly different frequencies are presented separately to the ears. The listener may perceive a rhythmic fluctuation at approximately the difference between them. In a 300/303 Hz pair, the carrier tones are 300 and 303 Hz and the nominal difference is 3 Hz.

The app uses conventional EEG-band labels only as orientation: roughly delta 1–4 Hz, theta 4–8 Hz, alpha 8–13 Hz and beta 13–30 Hz. A matching binaural difference does not prove that the brain will be forced into that oscillatory state or produce a particular psychological effect.

Why the default is 300 / 303

There is no well-established universal “ideal” carrier such as 300 or 400 Hz. The app chooses 300 Hz as an ordinary audible starting carrier and uses the requested 3 Hz separation. The user can change both.

A 2023 systematic review of binaural-beat EEG entrainment found inconsistent results across 14 studies, with substantial methodological heterogeneity. Other reviews report mixed or low-certainty results depending on outcome and protocol.
