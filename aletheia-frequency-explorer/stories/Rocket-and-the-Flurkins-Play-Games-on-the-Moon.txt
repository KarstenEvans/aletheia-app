Rocket and the Flurkins Play Games on the Moon

Rocket was an orange cat.

Well...

Rocket looked like an ordinary orange cat.

One night he was sitting on the windowsill staring at the Moon.

It looked big.
It looked bright.
And Rocket thought it looked like a brilliant place for a game of chase.

Then something went...

BEEP!
BEEP!
BLOOP!

A tiny silver flying saucer floated outside the window.

Inside was Rocket's alien friend, Bloop.

“Rocket!” called Bloop.
“Fancy a trip to the Moon?”

Rocket looked at the Moon.
Then at the saucer.
Then back at the Moon.

“Do they have snacks?” he asked.

“Of course,” said Bloop.

Rocket jumped straight in.

WHOOOOOSH!

The saucer flew up past the houses,
past the clouds,
past a very surprised aeroplane,
and all the way to the Moon.

When they landed, Rocket looked outside.

Five strange cats were waiting for him.

They had fluffy fur,
curly tails,
glowing whiskers,
and very big smiles.

They were Flurkins.

Moon Flurkins.

“Hello, Rocket!” they shouted.

One was blue.
One was purple.
One was green.
One had orange spots.
And one wore a tiny cape for absolutely no sensible reason.

“What do you do for fun?” Rocket asked.

The Flurkins looked at each other.

Then they all shouted:

“MOON CHASE!”

BOING!

The first Flurkin jumped over a crater.

BOING!

The second bounced over Bloop.

BOING!

The third bounced so high Rocket thought it might need a passport.

Rocket crouched down.

Wiggled his bottom.

And jumped.

BOOOOOING!

He flew right over all five Flurkins.

“Again!” cried Rocket.

Soon they were bouncing everywhere.

Over rocks.
Around craters.
Past the flying saucer.
Almost into the flying saucer.
And once, accidentally, over Bloop’s head.

Then one Flurkin rolled out a shiny silver ball on a long piece of string.

Every cat stopped.

Every tail twitched.

Every pair of eyes became enormous.

The ball moved.

Just a little.

POUNCE!

Six Flurkins and one Rocket leapt at it at the same time.

There was bouncing.
There was rolling.
There was one upside-down cat.
And there was Bloop shouting:

“WHO STOLE MY SHOE?”

Nobody knew.

After the game, they all sat on a moon rock and ate star-shaped biscuits.

Rocket had three.

Then four.

Then one that definitely belonged to somebody else.

Finally Bloop said,
“We should go home.”

Rocket looked at his new friends.

“Can I come back?”

“Of course!” said the Flurkins.

“Bring more fish treats!”

“And another string!”

“And Bloop’s other shoe!”

Rocket climbed back into the saucer.

WHOOOOOSH!

Soon he was home again.

He jumped onto the windowsill just before his human came into the room.

“Rocket,” she said,
“have you been sitting there all evening?”

Rocket looked very innocent.

He gave one tiny meow.

Then something rolled out from under his paw.

A glowing silver moon ball.

Rocket stared at it.

The ball twitched.

His eyes went enormous.

POUNCE!

Some cats dream about going to the Moon.

Rocket preferred bringing the games home.

The End
