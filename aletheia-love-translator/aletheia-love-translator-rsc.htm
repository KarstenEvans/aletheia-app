What is worth keeping?

Intent ≠ reception

You can care sincerely while sending a signal your partner barely notices.

Specific beats vague

“Be more loving” is fog. “Give me twenty phone-free minutes” is actionable.

Ask, don't mind-read

A profile starts a conversation. It does not prove what another person needs today.

Use a varied love diet

Do not starve four channels because one happened to score highest.

Evidence check

Short version: the five-language metaphor can be useful, but the strict “everyone has one primary language and matching it is the key” claim has weak support. Responsiveness, listening and broader caring behaviour are stronger foundations.
SourceWhy it matters
Impett, Park & Muise (2024)Review found weak support for the strongest love-language claims and proposed a broader “balanced diet” view of loving behaviours.
Preregistered 2025 studyFewer than half had an identifiable single primary language; broader satisfaction with loving behaviour tracked relationship quality.
Coy & RodriguezPartners can misjudge one another's preferences, supporting Aletheia's ask-and-calibrate rule.
Mostova et al. (2022)Found associations between preference/expression matching and satisfaction, so the literature is mixed rather than simply “debunked.”
Arican-Dinc & Gable, responsiveness reviewPerceived partner responsiveness centres on feeling understood, validated and cared for, a useful foundation for the translator.
High-quality listening reviewConnects good listening with perceived responsiveness. “Listen first” can be a communication preference separate from a love-language channel.
Seedall et al. (2024)Examines demand/withdraw interaction patterns in couple conflict. Aletheia uses this as a possible interaction-cycle concept, not a diagnosis.

New in v0.5: communication timing is not a love language

Immediate clarifier

Some people want to understand the problem now and naturally ask more questions. That can be care, anxiety, problem-solving, habit, or several other things. It is not automatically WORDS or ACTS.

Delayed processor

Some people need five or ten minutes before they can say what they mean. A short delay does not automatically equal avoidance or stonewalling.

The loop

If one asks harder while the other goes quieter, the pattern can amplify itself. A useful repair is often a pause with a return point: “Take ten minutes. Can we come back at 7:30?”

Aletheia rule

Describe the interaction before labelling either person. Test the hypothesis with both partners.

Free-first gestures T-)

The better free repair gifts are things like a handwritten note, making tea, doing a hated chore, picking a flower from your own garden, a photo, a playlist, a walk, or simply giving someone twenty uninterrupted minutes. T-)

📝 Notes

A sticky note, lunch-box message or tiny handwritten sentence can be powerful because it says “I thought about you when you weren't standing in front of me.”

🙂 Food faces

Turn an ordinary meal into a small affection signal with a smile, heart or silly arrangement.

☕ Acts

Tea made correctly, the hated chore quietly done, or one burden removed.

🚶 Time

A phone-free walk, a shared memory, a playlist or twenty undivided minutes.

Free-first rule: use your own flowers or ones you have permission to pick. Cemeteries are not an Aletheia-approved zero-cost flower shop. T-)

Cosy blankets, mutually wanted relaxation or affectionate closeness. Preference for touch is never blanket consent.
