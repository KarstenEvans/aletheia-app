HELP

CHECK: rename Ken and Barbie if wanted, select a person, and answer one question at a time. Switching people does not lose their answers.

Couple panel: shows progress, live scores and the answers already chosen. Tap a question in the trail to revisit it.

Talk timing: wanting an answer now versus needing five or ten minutes to formulate one is recorded separately from love-language preference. It is a communication pattern, not a diagnosis.

TRANSLATE: meaning comes first. The app states literal intent, possible undertext, what the other person may hear, then adapts delivery.

AI NUANCE: copies the profiles, timing and exact sentence into a careful prompt and opens ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude or DeepSeek in a separate window.

REPAIR: uses current state plus the receiver's profile as clues. Touch always requires consent; gifts never buy forgiveness.

Memory: this browser version uses session storage only so switching people or refreshing does not lose the current session. Closing the tab normally ends that session.
