HELP

CHECK: rename Ken and Barbie, choose a person and answer one question at a time. Switching people keeps their answers.

Talk timing: wanting an answer now versus needing five or ten minutes first is separate from love-language preference.

TRANSLATE: states literal meaning first, then possible undertext and a meaning-preserving delivery.

ANALYSE: looks at words and actions for possible interaction loops, warnings and a small next experiment. It describes patterns, not personality diagnoses.

REPAIR: checks state, accountability, changed behaviour and how the receiver can accept repair now.

AI NUANCE: copies both profiles, timing, response tendencies and the exact description into a prompt for the chosen AI.

Memory: session storage only. Closing the tab normally ends the app session.
