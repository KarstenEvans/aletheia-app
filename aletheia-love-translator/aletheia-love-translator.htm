    5. Free-first love gestures T-)

    The better free repair gifts are things like a handwritten note, making tea, doing a hated chore, picking a flower from your own garden, a photo, a playlist, a walk, or simply giving someone twenty uninterrupted minutes. T-)

    📝 Tiny note

    Hide a specific kind sentence in a lunch box, beside food, in a book or with something they use.

    🙂 Food-face diplomacy

    A smiley face, heart or silly pattern made from food says “I thought about you” without requiring a grand speech.

    ☕ Ordinary care

    Make the tea correctly, quietly remove a burden, or complete a genuinely disliked chore.

    🎵 Attention gifts

    A playlist, photo, shared-memory message, phone-free walk or twenty minutes of undivided attention.

    Free means no purchase is required, not creative acquisition. Pick flowers only from your own garden or somewhere you have permission. Cemeteries are not zero-cost florists. T-)

    Repair Dictionary

    WORDS

    Acknowledge, apologise, validate and reassure.

    ACTS

    Repair consequences and change behaviour. Apology repairs meaning; restitution repairs consequence.

    TIME

    Listen with attention, but do not force a conversation while someone is flooded.

    TOUCH

    Offer closeness, never assume it: “Would a hug help, or would you rather have space?”

    GIFTS

    A symbolic extra after accountability, not a forgiveness vending machine.

    SPACE + HUMOUR

    Useful modifiers when they reduce heat without dodging responsibility.

    STATE ≠ CHARACTER. Temporary stress, tiredness, anger or physiology is not a personality diagnosis.

    HELP

    The five channels

    WORDS sincere appreciation/reassurance · ACTS useful help/follow-through · GIFTS thoughtful symbols · TIME focused presence · TOUCH welcome affectionate contact, always governed by consent.

    Why no single fixed language?

    Aletheia reports a ranked profile because research does not strongly support forcing everybody into one permanent primary category.

    What is Repair?

    Repair handles what happens after intent and reception diverge: acknowledge the rupture, check state, translate the repair, fix consequences, verify whether it helped, and optionally remember the useful lesson.

    What if the message is angry, a boundary or a breakup?

    Meaning outranks styling. “We are over” must stay a breakup. “Do not touch me” must stay a boundary. The selected target style can only improve clarity, timing or practical delivery.
