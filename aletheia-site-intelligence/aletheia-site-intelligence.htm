What the research covers

Search questions

Google Trends, Bing research and useful related questions when real sources are accessible. Trend scores are not absolute search counts.

Competitor context

Compare public websites, topics and unanswered visitor questions. We cannot read competitors' private Search Console data.

AI visibility

Inspect actual supported AI citations or documented test prompts when possible. This does not reveal what all ChatGPT or Gemini users typed.

Demo scope: up to 5 keyword/question ideas, 1 competitor and 3 suggested improvements. Supporter handoff: up to 30, 3 competitors and 5 actions, subject to actual source access. No promise of paid data or guaranteed rankings.
