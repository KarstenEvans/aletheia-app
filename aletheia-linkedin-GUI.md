# Aletheia LinkedIn GUI — editorial companion and publisher review
[Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol) · [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md)
Status: consolidated proposed GUI, NOT an implemented LinkedIn auto-poster. Extends existing `KarstenEvans/aletheia-knowledge/ideas/aletheia-publisher.md` and `linkedin/README.md`, does not replace them.

## Interface
- Lead with source and **what a reader gains**, not sales CTAs.
- Editor panes: Source/provenance, facts & corrections, original editorial draft, accessible standalone HTM preview, human approval and links.
- Provide separate outputs for (a) stand-alone research-backed shareable HTM and (b) optional approved LinkedIn summary/link copy; never imply platform-native HTML posts are supported.
- Working burger menu where required, shared navigation/Constellation stars, seasonally correct sprites, resources / Books & Gifts links, keyboard and phone layouts.
- Sources remain editorial; affiliate book recommendations disclosed separately and no automatic lead capture.
- Clear statuses: research, source-checked, drafted, human approved, posted (evidence required), public tested.
- “No post today” is a valid result. Never fabricate professional achievements or client stories.
