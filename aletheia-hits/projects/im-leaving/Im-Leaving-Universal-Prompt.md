## COMPLETE CROSS-PROVIDER PROMPT
You are the music producer, arranger, lyric editor and vocal director for an **original song** called “I'm Leaving”. Your objective is to substantially improve the attached rough Version 7 MP3 while preserving its **dramatic storyline, clear opposing voice characters, insistent repeated words and eventual emotional release**. The reference mix is an arrangement template, *not* a demand to reproduce every tone or the current synthetic speech. Do not copy any existing commercial music or identifiable artist voice.

**Story and emotional truth:** A mature person in a tense professional meeting feels interrogated rather than heard. The other person keeps saying “I just want to summarise” while circling “social media” and “IT policy”, over and over. The speaker first tries to remain reasonable, then feels worn down by the same phrases; finally they set a boundary: “I'm leaving”. One sharp “Fuck... fuck off... off” represents a moment of exhausted frustration, **not** constant rage. The song should give listeners the sense that someone understands what it is like not to be heard. The final feeling should be *relief and quiet dignity*, not vengeance. The meeting is described subjectively and should not identify any real individuals.

**VoiceVibe: researched Maxi Jazz reference, realised as a distinct original narrator.** Design a new adult masculine English-speaking voice: warm low-mid speaking register, gently lived-in grain and breath, thoughtful, reassuring, quiet authority, a person who seems to have experienced hardship but retains humour and humanity. Almost all lyrics are **spoken in natural conversational rhythm**, not sung. Make words land ahead of or behind the beat, varying pacing, emphasis and pause naturally; keep consonants legible. No grandstage announcer, no generic rap flow, no theatrical growling, no robotic timing, no hard Auto-Tune. DO NOT clone Maxi Jazz, ingest Faithless vocals for likeness transfer, or reproduce his unique timbre/cadence. Make a *new performer* with the independent traits described.

**Second voice, mechanical antagonist:** A different original masculine voice, deliberately thin, unexpressive, synthetic, nagging and exactly repeated, with narrow-band EQ and occasional chopped echoes. It should sound like bureaucracy looping, not a sympathetic duet. Do not give it a sung melody. Repeat “I just want to summarise”, “social media”, and “IT policy” relentlessly. Each return should intrude on the narrator more; vary the backing texture, not the speaker's monotonous personality.

**HitsVibe: techniques studied in Faithless, translated into original music.** A spacious, emotional 1990s-to-2000s progressive-house / atmospheric dance arrangement with richer harmonic and rhythmic detail than V7: ~122–126 BPM, 4/4, warm layered electric piano and strings/organ, multiple slightly contrasting synth textures, organic plucks, small melodic countermotifs, syncopated bass line with rests, evolving ghost-note percussion and occasional real-world sound textures. Keep the pulse hypnotic but don't simply repeat the same four beats for three minutes. Musical parts must *enter and leave* thoughtfully; tiny new details should reward replay. Let the instrumental carry the song's melody while the voice speaks. Include a **long build / delayed payoff** inspired by the general tension-building craft documented in the Faithless HitsVibe: hint at the main riff early but **do not play it in full until late**. An interesting motif is earned through waiting, silence, and return. Avoid copying Faithless riffs, sounds or recordings.

**Influence weighting:** primarily the calm human storytelling + layered dance arrangement above; a secondary minimalist robot-repetition mechanism (the Kraftwerk HitsVibe), one or two playful gated voice fragments (the Daft Punk HitsVibe), and a touch of claustrophobic emotional contrast (Soft Cell HitsVibe). Do not make this a pastiche of four bands; build one original cohesive song.

**Architecture, NOT ordinary verse-chorus pop:** (1) sparse atmospheric intro; (2) narrator's measured story; (3) robotic “summarise” motif enters; (4) overlapping interruptions with bass/percussion changes; (5) a breath and a beatless break; (6) “I'm leaving” delivered as spoken decision with delayed echoes “leaving... leaving”; (7) the robot attempts another loop and fails; (8) a short genuine swear fragment; (9) late full original melodic release, warmer broader instruments; (10) a gentler spacious ending. A repeating spoken refrain can act as the chorus, but the narrator should **not sing a conventional chorus**. Keep the central lyric as editable original text.

**Editing priorities, in order:** 1. natural, convincing human narrator; 2. legibility/contrast of two voices; 3. musical layers, rhythmic interplay and surprise; 4. delayed melodic payoff; 5. emotionally satisfying final silence and afterglow. Preserve the refrains and distinctive lyric fragments. If your system cannot process the supplied MP3 or full length, say so, then use the supplied 59-second segment and text as a seed. **Do not claim a faithful reference transformation unless audio was actually analysed.**

**Negative prompt:** no copied Faithless track, no sampled or recognisable Maxi Jazz voice, no generic stadium EDM riser every eight bars, no monotone narrator, no constant four-note arpeggio, no heavy pitch correction, no female narrator or meeting voice in this iteration, no cheery sung chorus, no invented meeting facts, no copyrighted lyric quotation.

**Deliverable:** an original 3–4-minute track if audio generation is available, along with its exact lyric sheet and a brief changelog of what you kept/changed from V7. If text-only, deliver a structured arrangement/lyrics/prompt ready to paste into a music generator and **state clearly that no audio has been produced**. Ask for no payment and do not use more generation credits than necessary.
