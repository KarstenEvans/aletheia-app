## Refrain lock
Keep the exact creator-approved core words prominent:
“And social media, social media and IT policy. IT policy. I just want to summarise social media, IT policy. I want to summarise.”
The arrangement may break these up rhythmically, but must not replace them with a conventional sung pop chorus.

## Emotional safety
This is an artistic presentation of frustration and boundary-setting, not a plan to threaten, harass or harm anyone. Keep the lyric ambiguous enough to stand on its own as a universal story about being unheard.
