# Aletheia Hits | Portable Song Development System

[Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol) (sources, provenance and uncertainty) | [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md) (optional considerate humour)

## Purpose

**Let the human do the directing, and let AI do the musical walking.** A free-first, provider-independent process for developing better original songs *before* burning Suno/other generation credits. This is a portable AI-handoff spec, NOT an operational music model or browser TTS engine.

## One-song workflow (KISS)

1. **Story:** Describe the listener's emotional situation and what they should feel (recognition, relief, humour, resolve). Distinguish words actually spoken from fictional artistic interpretation.
2. **Reference research:** Select one or more HitsVibes. Record accessible sources and make an *observed / inferred / subjective* distinction. Do not use the band's name as an instruction to replicate a recording.
3. **Narrator:** Choose or design an original VoiceVibe. Narrator and backing refrain may have completely different vocal behaviours.
4. **Lyric workshop:** User retains line-level control. Map full phrases, interrupted words, echoes, micro-repeats, breath pauses, stressed words and dramatic silences. Label repeated hooks as refrains even without a pop chorus.
5. **Arrangement map:** Plan 8/16-bar scenes, instruments entering/leaving, bass variations, acoustic/digital contrasts, delayed melodic payoff, microdetail and breaks. An interesting build is *not* maximum loudness all the time.
6. **Free review:** Read lyrics aloud without music; test cadence and dramatic direction. Audition a rough local instrumental or user-provided reference where available. Compare A/B prompts and fix unclear instructions before external render.
7. **Handoff:** Export (a) 1 compact style prompt, (b) section-marked original lyrics, (c) optional advanced production/voice brief, (d) revision delta. Copy manually to a user-selected music service. Record service, licence, output and costs. No API token in public HTML.
8. **Listen + reflect:** Human scores engagement, vocal honesty, groove, repeat discovery, emotional release and unwanted generative artifacts. Revise *one dimension at a time*. Keep original assets and revision history.

## 'Hit' characteristics are hypotheses, not a magic algorithm

Evaluate: emotional recognition; distinct narrative or proposition; phrase-level memorability; beat and bass danceability; surprise/anticipation; dynamics and space; repeat-listen layers; vocal presence; memorable ending. A hook may be a *spoken phrase, texture or riff*. The user, not the assistant, is the final judge.

## Suggested project assets

`projects/<slug>/lyrics.md`, `production-brief.md`, `prompt-for-ai.md`, `revisions.md`, optional user-owned recorded stems. Keep `hits/` for music construction research and `../aletheia-voice-studio/voice-vibes/` for vocal identity design; never duplicate a voice model in Hits.

## Ethics and reliability

- Independent original songs; no wholesale extraction/redistribution of copyrighted commercial stems or reproduction of melodies/lyrics.
- Artist-inspired study should translate into *general characteristics*, not the recreation or implied endorsement of a recognizable artist voice.
- Consent and rights gate for voice reference recordings, including samples from commercial MP3s. Avoid treating source separation as permission to clone a performer.
- Public project assets must not contain real colleagues' identities, private personnel records or private recordings unless explicitly approved for publication.
- False claims of audio generation, live compatibility, actual listening, library import, or tested model quality are prohibited.
- All Markdown exports generated by this system must include both protocol links near the start.

## Immediate demo

Use [I'm Leaving](projects/im-leaving/Im-Leaving-Universal-Prompt.md). Its narrator and mechanical meeting voice deliberately contrast; the refrain is a comic, claustrophobic mechanism rather than a standard chorus.

## Unbuilt / optional

Local WAV/stem preview, editable timing grid, real voice synthesis, pitch/formant processing, neural music models and direct provider adapters are **proposals**, not shipped features here. Use external generators only at the user's choice and expense. Avoid mandatory subscriptions.
