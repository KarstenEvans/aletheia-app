## Important distinction

HitsVibe describes **musical construction**: harmonic and rhythmic strategies, timbral palette, build, breaks, hooks, repetition, expressive lyric-writing, sources and confidence. VoiceVibe describes **vocal performance**: register, intimacy, delivery, cadence, emotion and consent-safe sound design. Aletheia Hits may link to, but does not copy, Aletheia Voice Studio's synthesis or the existing Writing Vibe engine.

Artist names identify **research references**, not a licence to copy recordings, melody, lyrics, or uniquely identifiable vocal identity. Use design traits in a novel composition. No guarantee of commercial success or virality.
