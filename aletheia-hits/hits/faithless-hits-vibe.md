# Faithless HitsVibe

[Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol) (evidence, provenance, uncertainty) | [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md) (optional considerate humour)

## Identity
Faithless combine Sister Bliss's layered dance arrangements, Rollo Armstrong's production and Maxi Jazz's reflective spoken-word writing. *Insomnia*, *Salva Mea*, *God Is a DJ*, *Mass Destruction* are research references only. **A HitsVibe is an analysis of musical mechanisms, not an instruction to copy a band's work.**

## Verified technique inventory
- Sister Bliss said she **held the major Insomnia keyboard riff until late**, inspired by Underworld's long builds. The nine-minute album version was initially criticised by radio programmers for having “no hook and no chorus.” This is true of *Insomnia*, not every Faithless track. [Guardian interview](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2020/oct/19/faithless-how-we-made-insomnia-sister-bliss-maxi-jazz).
- Sister Bliss described mixing JV-1080 digital textures with different JUNO-106 analogue synths and samples. *God Is a DJ* held its SH-101 bass line back before its full release; sounds were layered with an ear to unpredictability and texture. [Roland interview](https://articles.roland.com/faithless-breaking-down-classic-tracks-with-sister-bliss/).
- Sister Bliss describes the group's territory as **poetry, electronics and melody**. The speech supplies a rhythmic narrative while the arrangement provides the melodic expansion. [XS Noize interview](https://www.xsnoize.com/interview-to-me-the-music-doesnt-live-and-breathe-until-it-has-an-audience-a-chat-with-faithless-sister-bliss/).
- Maxi Jazz described drawing *Insomnia* details from toothache, sleeplessness and lived electricity-meter problems. *Mass Destruction* is an example of social commentary. [Guardian interview](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2020/oct/19/faithless-how-we-made-insomnia-sister-bliss-maxi-jazz) · [Double J](https://www.abc.net.au/listen/doublej/music-reads/features/faithless-sister-bliss-interview-maxi-jazz-dj-australia-2023/103233708).

## Original transferable arrangement rules
1. **Groove moves:** dance kick; syncopated bass with rests; shuffled and ghost-note percussion; deliberate fills, subdivisions and small timing variations. Don't leave a single 'doot-doot' loop unchanged throughout.
2. **Layer with space:** warm electric piano, wide strings/pads, short organic/plucked counter-rhythm, distant atmospheres and one small answer-melody. Introduce, subtract and modify layers over 8–16 bars rather than piling them all on.
3. **Wait:** imply an important melody with incomplete fragments; introduce its complete statement much later. Build curiosity and release through dynamic and harmonic contrast, not just volume.
4. **Words speak:** intimate, patient, mature first-person narrator speaking rhythmically with hesitations, internal rhymes and unforced philosophical turns. Give instruments the sustained tune. Conventional sung chorus optional; repeating short phrases can be dramatic refrains.
5. **Earn empathy:** serious themes can still feel uplifting when the narrator sounds grounded. This is an artistic hypothesis, **not** a guarantee of virality.
6. **Return changed:** repeated words remain fixed while accompaniment, filtering, placement and emotional stakes vary. A quiet breakdown before a decisive phrase is a deliberate weapon.

## I'm Leaving application
Narrator = distinct, independently created older-leaning male storyteller, calm but increasingly worn down. Meeting voice = deliberately irritating male synth/robot, phrases “I just want to summarise / social media / IT policy.” Make repetition itself the conflict. Narrator's final “I'm leaving” lands in silence, then “leaving... leaving” moves behind the beat before a warm delayed instrumental payoff. “Fuck... fuck off... off” is a rare lapse in restraint, not continuous aggression.

## Keep original
No recognisable copy of Maxi Jazz's voice, no copied melodies, masters, sampled commercial vocals or substantial lyric quotation. A tempo is a production choice, not a historically documented universal band signature.
