# Soft Cell HitsVibe

[Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol) (evidence, provenance, uncertainty) | [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md) (optional considerate humour)

## Evidence
Soft Cell's Dave Ball described the collision of early Korg synths and a Synclavier in *Tainted Love*, while Marc Almond recalled experimental electro songs about consumerism and strange processed vocal effects. [Guardian interview](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2017/mar/20/how-we-made-tainted-love-mark-almond-dave-ball-interview). Soft Cell juxtaposed comparatively minimal electronics with theatrical, emotionally exposed singing, sometimes abrasiveness and dark satire. [Guardian profile](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2011/jul/18/marc-almond-interview-ten-plagues) · [Dave Ball / Marc Almond interview](https://www.on-magazine.co.uk/interview/marc-almond-dave-ball-soft-cell/).

## Original transferable HitsVibe
- **Cold arrangement, hot emotion:** combine sparse, hard-edged sequencer bass/drum sounds with expressive and sometimes imperfect human delivery.
- Dramatic pauses, vocal desperation or occasionally a shout can become focal points because the surrounding texture stays restrained.
- Lyrics may use uncomfortable concrete situations, ironic juxtaposition, vulnerability, black humour and a memorable simple phrase.
- Use minor-key pressure, claustrophobic close sound, then a sudden break or widening of the sonic space.

## I'm Leaving example
Reserve slight *Sex Dwarf*-associated claustrophobia and theatrical unease for the spiralling questions, but keep the narrator calm and credible. One sharp swear line carries more force than constant shouting.

## Boundaries
No recognisable copying of Soft Cell compositions, performances, melodies or samples. Don't turn an emotional spoken narrator into a parody of Marc Almond.
