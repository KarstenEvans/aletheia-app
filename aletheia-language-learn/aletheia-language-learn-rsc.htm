Design rule: show Thai script first. Romanisation supports the learner but should not become the language.

What the Thai tutor should notice

  • mid, low, falling, high and rising tone categories;
  • vowel length as a meaningful contrast;
  • tone spelling depends on more than the visible tone mark;
  • classifiers and sentence particles matter in natural use;
  • connected speech is not identical to isolated textbook tone contours;
  • a transcript cannot prove that a learner pronounced every tone correctly.

Useful research/reference routes: Thai orthography and tone spelling · vowel length · tone and vowel quantity.
