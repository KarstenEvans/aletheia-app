## Thai learning design notes

- Thai has five standard tone categories: mid, low, falling, high and rising.
- Written tone marks do not map one-to-one to spoken tone in every syllable.
- Tone determination depends on factors including initial consonant class, syllable type, tone mark and vowel length.
- Vowel length is contrastive in Thai.
- Romanisation should support the learner, not replace Thai script.
- Tone/pronunciation should not be declared correct from transcript-only input.

## Resource rule

Language-learning resources should return the learner to doing:
- speak;
- type;
- handwrite;
- match;
- sort;
- reconstruct;
- retrieve;
- role-play;
- teach back.

Do not turn the resource page into a passive link warehouse.
