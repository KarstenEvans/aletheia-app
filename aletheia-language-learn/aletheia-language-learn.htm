Thai is not an afterthought

ภาษาไทย

Thai lessons should show Thai script first, use romanisation only as support, teach the five tone categories and vowel length gradually, and introduce tone spelling through small consonant-class / live-dead / tone-mark / vowel-length pattern exercises. Classifiers and particles are taught in real situations rather than as a grammar avalanche.

Audio honesty: a transcript matching the intended word does not prove the learner's tones or vowel lengths were correct.
