**Original fictional voice:** Weathered Frontier Marshal. A creative homage to broadly observed performance qualities, not a reconstruction or authorised endorsement of an identifiable performer. **Public WAV:** not yet uploaded.

## Voice-design instruction

An original mature masculine voice for a weather-beaten American frontier storyteller. Broad low baritone with substantial chest resonance, a dry forward/nasal edge and gentle gravel at the end of phrases. Stylised mid-century American Western drawl, broadly Plains/Southwestern rather than a modern Deep South accent. Let key vowels open and linger; relax casual consonants, then land command words with definite clarity. Rhythm is distinctive: one or two measured words, a half-beat of silence, a longer easy phrase, and a low deliberate finish. Convey the authority of an unhurried trail marshal who listens before speaking. Insert occasional wry humour and quiet protective warmth. When annoyed, tighten into a short rasp, never a theatrical shout. Make pauses feel chosen, not robotic. Slightly rueful, grounded, capable of a small amused breath. Avoid generic cowboy slogans, comic drawl exaggeration, thick Southern parody, famous catchphrases or replication of an identifiable film actor. A new independent fictional voice.
