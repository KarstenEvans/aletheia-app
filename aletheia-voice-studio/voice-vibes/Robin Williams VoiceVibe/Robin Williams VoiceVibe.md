## Voice-design instruction

An original quicksilver adult masculine storyteller: warm, playful and empathic, with a neutral American English accent, softly textured middle speaking register, and a wide expressive pitch range. Thoughts form surprising associations, little melodic climbs, abrupt changes of speed, brief delighted pauses and affectionate curiosity. Let comic energy bubble in short bursts before returning to sincere thoughtful conversation. Combine gentle breathiness in reflective phrases with clear agile articulation when enthusiasm rises. The narrator notices small human incongruities, sometimes gives ordinary objects improbable motivations, and can move from a fanciful thought to quiet kindness without losing credibility. Keep timing flexible and natural, with strongly contrasted speeds rather than permanently hyperactive chatter. This is an entirely new fictional voice; do not imitate a specific performer, copyrighted character or recognisable catchphrase.
