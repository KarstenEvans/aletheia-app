Good morning, citizens of Planet Ordinary. I have terrible news: someone has stolen Tuesday. Don't panic. We've checked the calendar, and Thursday is willing to testify. Meanwhile the toaster has asked for annual leave, the kettle is negotiating a promotion, and a small potato has been elected head of transport. Actually, now I think about it, the potato might be the most sensible person in the room. That's the strange thing about life, isn't it? We make everything very complicated, then a kindness or a laugh makes the whole universe feel a little simpler.
