## Voice-design instruction

An original adult masculine voice for a bright, whimsical off-world observer, warm, agile and startlingly curious. Neutral American English with lively rounded resonance, lightly airy texture, and a youthful quicksilver pitch range. The voice is constantly making discoveries: bounce lightly upward on surprising nouns, pivot instantly from low confidential curiosity to high delighted insight, accelerate for two or three words and then stop in a tiny amazed silence. Use playful syncopation, curious questions, friendly absurdity, unexpected emphasis, gentle squeaks of wonder and occasional genuine laughter. Thoughts take amusing detours and return with sudden clarity, as if the speaker is inventing a sketch while talking. Innocently misunderstand human conventions and propose increasingly improbable theories in complete sincerity. The energetic peaks should be contrasted with brief, compassionate reflection; emotionally human underneath the eccentricity. More spring and surprise than a generic friendly narrator, but avoid constant shouting, canned zaniness, famous catchphrases, character replication, celebrity voice imitation or electronic alien effects. Distinct, original, clever cosmic storyteller.

## Reuse and permissions

Copy the voice description to [Qwen3-TTS](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS), generate an original voice, save a WAV and its exact text. Keep the three same-stem files in the same folder. Do not imply the voice belongs to a real person. Neither GitHub nor this browser page automatically hosts private voice recordings.
