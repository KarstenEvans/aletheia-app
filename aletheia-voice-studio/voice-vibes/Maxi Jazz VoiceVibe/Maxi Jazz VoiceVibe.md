## Sources and observations
- In [The Guardian's “How we made Insomnia”](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2020/oct/19/faithless-how-we-made-insomnia-sister-bliss-maxi-jazz), Maxi Jazz describes writing direct, personal words quickly for electronic music, rooted in lived circumstances.
- Sister Bliss describes Faithless's mixture of poetry, electronics and melody in [XS Noize](https://www.xsnoize.com/interview-to-me-the-music-doesnt-live-and-breathe-until-it-has-an-audience-a-chat-with-faithless-sister-bliss/).
- A [2024 Guardian retrospective](https://www.theguardian.com/music/article/2024/jun/11/faithless-interview-sister-bliss-maxi-jazz) describes his *Insomnia* performance as an anxiety-laden monologue carried into joyous club energy.
- **Listener's subjective aim:** relaxed, understood and happy despite serious lyrical themes; a mature, travelled, credible speaker with sorrows and humour behind the voice. This is a desired experience, not a measured personality fact about the singer.

## Transferable voice-design prompt: an original “Experienced Soul”
Create an entirely **new**, mature adult male UK English spoken-word narrator. Low-mid speaking register, not cartoon-deep. Warmth, clear consonants, natural breath and a slightly weathered edge. Intimate and unhurried, speaking as if to one listener across a table. He sounds emotionally intelligent because of timing and emphasis, not imitation of any real person's accent, grain, timing signature or delivery. Let serious lines arrive quietly, allow human timing imperfections, dry warmth, swallowed pauses, small late stresses and subtle inner rhymes. The narrator's *speech* has rhythmic flow but mostly **does not sing**. A melodic line behind him comes from the instrumentation.

Performance direction: [1] stable, conversational description; [2] slightly more breath and shorter sentences as the interruptions continue; [3] fatigue and firm boundaries on “I'm leaving”; [4] one sharply accented profane line when necessary; [5] exhausted but relieved final fading words. No shouting throughout. Think humanity, dignity, calm confidence and frailty under pressure.

**Do not:** clone or sound indistinguishable from Maxi Jazz, use an original Faithless vocal as a cloning reference, demand that a model sing all lyrics, add exaggerated elderly accents, force a synthetic Auto-Tune effect, or remove every natural hesitation.

## Sonics for an authorised generated or recorded original voice
Use low compression, a little de-essing, very gentle warm EQ, small room ambience, modest consonant clarity. Retain breaths and dynamic range; avoid aggressive pitch correction, formant shifting, buzz or identical timing. A separated synthetic meeting character may use telephone EQ, gating and repeated delays.
