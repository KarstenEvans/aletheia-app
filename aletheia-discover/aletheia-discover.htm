What this pilot does

It creates source-search links for the place you choose and an Aletheia-style research brief in your chosen answer language. Your inputs stay in this page unless you open an external search or copy them to an AI. There is no location permission, account, subscription, live results feed, automated newsletter or affiliate link.

Search engines and AI providers have their own privacy practices. Events, opening hours, prices and news may change; use the original source and its date. Search snippets are leads, not independently verified facts.
