Aletheia Decision Check

Nine questions to make assumptions, evidence and uncertainty visible. The tool helps structure a decision. It does not make the decision for you.

1Define the decision

What actual choice or action must be made?

2Identify assumptions

What must be true for the current plan to work?

3Gather evidence

Record observations and sources. Separate fact, inference and forecast.

4Challenge the strongest assumption

What evidence would make you change your mind?

5Pre-mortem

Assume this failed. What plausible causes, warning signs and mitigations explain the failure?

6Find missing information

What do you still not know, and could it change the decision?

7Reduce alternatives

Remove duplicates and weak variants. Keep materially different survivors.

8Show remaining uncertainty

What disagreement, ambiguity or evidence limit remains?

9STOP / human decision

State the current decision point. "Enough" means enough for this task, not certainty.
