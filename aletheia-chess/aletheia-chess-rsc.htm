Chess sets: choose by how they will be used

Best first set

Weighted Staunton club set

Clear pieces, a roll-up board and spare queens make the most useful set for learning, clubs and travel. Look for a king around 3.5–3.75 inches and matching square size.

Alice through the chessboard

Through the Looking-Glass, rather than the first Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland, is built around chess. The countryside is an enormous board. Alice replaces Lily as the White Queen’s Pawn on the second rank, travels towards the eighth rank, becomes a Queen and helps produce checkmate.

The original game

White Pawn to play and win in eleven moves

Lewis Carroll printed a starting diagram and move schedule. It is a literary journey expressed through legal chess events, not a normal competitive game: the sides do not always alternate strictly and the story’s “castling” of three queens means entering the palace.

Read the illustrated public-domain book

Load the original position

Alice starting-position FEN

6n1/8/2K5/5N2/4k3/8/3Pq3/2Q2R2 w - - 0 1

Paste this into Aletheia Chess’s FEN box to display Carroll’s starting diagram. Alice is the White Pawn on d2. This recreates the position only; the app’s normal turn checker cannot reproduce the complete literary schedule because Carroll does not alternate Red and White consistently.

Future side idea

Follow Alice mode

A guided story mode could load the FEN, highlight Alice’s route from d2 to d8, promote her to Queen, and display the matching chapter encounter at each stage. It should be labelled a literary reconstruction rather than an ordinary legal chess game.

What the pieces looked like

Tenniel’s living chess characters

In the original illustrations, the Red and White royalty, knights, pawns and castles are characterful figures rather than plain Staunton men. Alice herself is the pawn-character, later crowned as a queen. Modern themed sets usually map the Mad Hatter, White Rabbit, Cheshire Cat, queens and other characters onto ordinary chess roles.

For people who like chess and cats

Cat-and-chess gifts for humans

A cat knocking over a king, sleeping on a board or acting as an unimpressed arbiter can work on mugs, prints, shirts and cushions.

Pet-safe photo idea

Use a lightweight breakaway collar charm, chess-pattern blanket or a board placed nearby. Photograph the cat choosing whether to participate—as cats traditionally do.

Avoid chess-piece costumes

Do not recommend restrictive hats, capes, elastic loops, dangling pieces or anything that impairs movement, hearing, sight, breathing or grooming. Never leave a dressed pet unattended.
