Aletheia Site Audit

Is your site reachable, understandable and genuinely helpful? Start with HTTPS, redirects and discovery files, then inspect content quality, optional search/AI visibility and proportionate improvements.

STAGE 1

Reachability gate

HTTPS/HTTP, redirect paths, DNS/TLS clues, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt and source-vs-AI retrieval problems. Do not call an AI safety refusal a site-side HTTP failure.

STAGES 2–4

Discovery and quality

Crawlability, titles, canonicals, readable answers, accessibility, source links, useful internal navigation and disclosure. Competitor/advertorial review is optional.

OPTIONAL STAGE 3A

Search Intelligence

Google Trends, Bing Keyword Research, owner-authorised Search Console/Bing reports and AI citations when actually accessible. Figures retain source, place, period and metric definitions.

For a real audit, give a capable AI the linked Markdown and your domain. Capability gaps must be disclosed rather than simulated.

What will be in the report?

  1. Target, date/time, reachability gate, tested routes and evidence status.
  2. Discovery files, HTML/indexing/technical issues, site-quality observations.
  3. Optional Search Intelligence: exact sources used/unavailable, Trends relative interest, Bing keyword questions, first-party metrics (only when authorised) and grouped AI citations (not exact user prompts).
  4. Potential reader questions and content gaps, optional affiliate suitability, smallest reversible improvements and a dated retest plan.
  5. A separate <hostname>-results.md evidence receipt; suggested actions are not marked as completed changes.

No automatic advertising spend, account access, subscription, tracking installation, publication or guessed search volumes.
