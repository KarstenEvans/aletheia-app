01. Search, trends and keyword research

These are the starting points. Search demand is an editorial clue, not evidence that a product is good, a local event exists or every popular term deserves an article.

FREE · PUBLIC

Compare interest in topics or exact terms across countries, regions and periods. Useful for seasonal patterns, related topics and rising searches.

Measure: sampled, normalised interest (0–100), not absolute search counts.

05. A practical affiliate-marketing method

The useful part of the public Revenue Tactics syllabus is its planning sequence, not the courses or high-pressure sales material: understand an audience, create useful original pages, check reachability, measure and improve.

  1. Start with a reader's question.

    Research the exact task and location. Do not pick a subject only because it has a big commission.

  2. Check first-party sources.

    Verify dates, products, policies, live links and claims. Keep what the seller says separate from independently tested facts.

  3. Write the useful answer first.

    Provide clear headings, steps, limitations, practical alternatives, mobile readability and related questions.

  4. Add a relevant offer last.

    Offer a genuine, optional complement; include free alternatives and a visible affiliate disclosure.

  5. Check trust and terms.

    Look at renewal charges, refunds, stock, UK availability, exaggerated income/health claims, merchant identity and approval.

  6. Measure and revise.

    Use comparable time periods and metrics. Correct errors, remove dead links and stop recommending offers that no longer fit.

What makes a useful landing or resource page?

A direct answer at the top, a meaningful title, readable original explanation, small related FAQs where genuinely helpful, credible sources, descriptive internal links, accessible media, working canonicals/sitemap entries, review date, clear next step and unambiguous commercial disclosure. Useful even if nobody buys anything.

How can Aletheia Publisher use this?

Record idea → real reader question → research receipt → draft → verification → duplicate/rights check → human approval → manual or authorised publication → live URL receipt → measured review. Include a legitimate NO POST outcome. A popular keyword is not a reason to invent content or publish repetitive AI filler.

How can Aletheia Trust Check use this?

Identify seller claims and affiliate incentives, verify independently where possible, distinguish advertising from evidence, inspect checkout/subscription/refund terms, and avoid turning one anecdote or review score into a business verdict. Affiliate reviews are not automatically independent reviews.

UK disclosures and visitor tracking

The UK Advertising Standards Authority expects affiliate advertising to be identifiable. Put a clear, prominent disclosure close to relevant recommendations. Review current ASA/CAP guidance ↗. Treat tracking pixels, analytics, cookies and session recordings according to current ICO storage-and-access guidance ↗; free pricing does not imply consent.

Paid products mentioned in marketing-tool lists

AdPlexity, Anstrex, Voluum, Convertri, ActiveCampaign and paid-ad networks such as Taboola, MGID or Revcontent are optional commercial categories, not dependencies for this guide. Our free-first path uses official Google/Bing tools, static HTML and permitted manual exports. Nothing here initiates a subscription or paid campaign.
