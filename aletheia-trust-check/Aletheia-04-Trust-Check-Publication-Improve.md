## Result

The actual .htm app and portable .md now support pasted posts, claims, messages, names and links. One Trust Check button copies the full checking method plus the user's task and opens one selected AI. Paste and Send there. The page does not manufacture a verdict or pretend to browse.

The HTML includes the complete current Markdown, so downloading the method and preparing a check do not require fetching another file. The .md can be attached or pasted into any capable AI independently of the HTML.

FACTS mode reports individual claims as SUPPORTED, MISLEADING, CONTRADICTED, UNVERIFIED or OPINION/PREDICTION, with sources and limits. Entity/offer checks retain the established qualitative trust indication separately.
