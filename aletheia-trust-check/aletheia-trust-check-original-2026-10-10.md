# Aletheia Trust Check

**Type:** Optional Aletheia add-on  
**Purpose:** Add evidence-aware trust checking to another Aletheia search without replacing that search.

## STARTUP / COMPOSITION
When this file accompanies another Aletheia task or search, do not show HELP first and do not restart the task. Run the original task first, then apply Trust Check to businesses, services, products, claims or organisations that materially affect the answer.

## TRUST CHECK
1. Confirm identity and avoid similarly named organisations.
2. Prefer official information, regulators, registers, professional bodies and primary documents.
3. Check recognised review platforms and independent sources.
4. Look for recurring complaint and praise themes, not isolated anecdotes.
5. Consider severity, recency, sample size and cross-source agreement.
6. Look for suspicious review signals such as sudden bursts, repetitive wording or implausible reviewer behaviour.
7. Check complaint responses where visible.
8. Compare marketing claims with independent evidence.
9. Use Reddit/forums/GitHub only where relevant and distinguish community experience from verified fact.
10. State important uncertainty and what could not be checked.

Do not use a fixed one-star-review percentage as an automatic fraud/trust verdict.

## OUTPUT
Keep the main answer readable. Add a compact trust section only where material:
- Trust indication: reassuring / mixed / caution / insufficient evidence
- Confidence: high / medium / low
- strongest positive evidence
- strongest negative evidence
- recurring complaint themes
- suspicious-review indicators, if any
- unresolved points
- sources and date checked

Do not invent a score or evidence when the information does not support one.

## HELP
Explain briefly that Trust Check cross-checks important businesses, services, products or claims against independent evidence and flags unresolved concerns.
