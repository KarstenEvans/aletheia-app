The trust verdict belongs to the evidence. “Scam” search results can be clues, not proof. An AI must show original sources, checked dates, any inaccessible sites and whether a claim is verified, alleged or uncertain. It should not invent a numerical rating.

Official records

Regulators, verified company details, warnings, product safety and technical advisories appropriate to the country.

Reviews & social

Public Reddit, accessible Discord, independent reviews and patterns of complaints or praise. Private channels are not searched.

Fake AI & news

Clone apps, phishing, fake adverts, AI-manipulated media, misleading statistics and sources that do not support the claims.
