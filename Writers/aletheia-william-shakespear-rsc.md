## Coverage and exclusions
The accompanying CSV is a **selected 14-title research catalogue**: the eight user-supplied titles plus six comparison works/collections. It is not a claim of a complete 41-book bibliography. Each row identifies genre, approximate composition and/or publication, attribution caveat, an authoritative title/source URL and a **Bookshop.org UK search URL**, not an edition-specific purchase link. ISBN is intentionally blank: a canonical early modern work has many modern editions, each with its own ISBN. Retail listings and availability were not independently verified. Do not invent them.

No unauthorised full-text mirrors, Scribd/Everand copies, social-post attribution or modern edition copyright assumptions are included. Use Scribd/Everand only if a clearly attributed, authorised public excerpt can be checked. No such excerpts were used in this research. All cited Folger passage pages were accessible; the Oxford Academic full chapter was not. Wikipedia source links provided by the user were treated as leads only.
