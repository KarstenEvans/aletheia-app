🎬 CHECK THIS VIDEO

Investigate the media, the alleged event and whether synthetic/fictional content was disclosed. This browser page prepares an AI research request; it does not watch or authenticate a video.

HELP: How to check a video (read this first)
  1. Enter a link, a caption or your description of the alleged event. One field is enough.
  2. Choose your preferred AI, then select Copy verification request & open AI. Paste the copied request into that chat.
  3. If the AI cannot open the video, attach screenshots or the clip yourself. Ask it to separate what it inspected from what the story claims, then review the source-linked Truth Card.

This is a research helper, not an automatic fake detector. AI-generated speech, dramatic music or missing Content Credentials alone do not establish fabrication. Mark unsupported cases UNVERIFIED.

HANDOFF, not CONNECTED AI. Paste and send in the chosen AI. If the post is login-protected, provide the caption or screenshots manually in that chat. Aletheia does not upload files or bypass access restrictions.

UNVERIFIED: no video has been analysed.

What this app does not do

No paywall bypass. No copied full articles. No political winner or source ideology score. Source count is not treated as independent confirmation if several outlets appear to derive from the same reporting lineage.
