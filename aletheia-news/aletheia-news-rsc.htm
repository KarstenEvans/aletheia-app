Bookshop: understanding news, evidence and misinformation

Bookshop.org UK has an official affiliate programme. Standard affiliates currently advertise a 10% commission. The links here are deliberately untracked until the real Aletheia/Swindon.org.uk affiliate link is available.

Calling Bullshit

Carl T. Bergstrom & Jevin D. West. Practical tools for questioning misleading statistics, graphs, hype and apparently impressive claims.

Bookshop.org UK ↗

Living with Misinformation

Aimee Hourigan, Tanya Notley, T. J. Thomson, Sora Park & Michael Dezuanni. A 2026 media-literacy book drawing on a large research study of how adults encounter misinformation.

Bookshop.org UK ↗

We Are Bellingcat

Eliot Higgins. Open-source investigation, verification and following digital evidence back toward its source.

Find on Bookshop.org UK ↗

News and How to Use It

Alan Rusbridger. A readable guide to how contemporary news works and how readers can navigate it.

Find on Bookshop.org UK ↗

The Elements of Journalism

Bill Kovach & Tom Rosenstiel. A useful background text on verification, independence and the role of journalism.

Find on Bookshop.org UK ↗
