# Aletheia Make a Will — Free Resources and Reading

**Version:** 0.1 · **Jurisdiction:** England and Wales unless marked otherwise · **Research checked:** 18 November 2026  
**Purpose:** One uncluttered companion list for **Make a Will**, **Family Handover**, **DIY Probate**, and **Build the Family Legacy**. Free official resources first; books last. This is not tax or legal advice.

## 1. Make a Will — free official help

- [GOV.UK: Make a will](https://www.gov.uk/make-will) — legal requirements and subjects to include.
- [GOV.UK: Signing and witnessing](https://www.gov.uk/make-will/make-sure-your-will-is-legal) — adult, writing, voluntariness, capacity, two witnesses and signing rules.
- [MoneyHelper: Writing a will and the options](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/family-and-care/death-and-bereavement/how-to-write-a-will-and-how-much-does-it-cost) — independent comparison of DIY, will writers and solicitors.
- [MoneyHelper: Using a solicitor](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/family-and-care/death-and-bereavement/using-a-solicitor-to-write-your-will) — how to find professional help and what to ask.
- [Will Aid 2026](https://www.willaid.org.uk/) — participating solicitors offer basic wills without their ordinary fee in November, with voluntary suggested charity donations. **Availability and suitability vary**.
- [Free Wills Month](https://freewillsmonth.org.uk/) — basic solicitor wills at selected locations during March/October, normally for people aged 55+. **Check location and appointment availability**.
- [SRA solicitor register](https://www.sra.org.uk/consumers/register/) — verify regulatory status, not quality endorsement.
- [HMCTS will storage](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/store-a-will-with-the-probate-service/how-to-store-a-will-with-the-probate-service) — optional official storage for the signed original; check fee.

**DIY template warning:** There is no general government-issued, universally suitable "sign here" will form. An online draft is not valid until the legal requirements are met; unusual families and trusts need specialist drafting.

## 2. Family Handover — free organisation tools

- [GOV.UK: What to do after someone dies](https://www.gov.uk/after-a-death) — registration and early tasks.
- [GOV.UK: Tell Us Once](https://www.gov.uk/after-a-death/organisations-you-need-to-contact-and-tell-us-once) — notifies most government bodies; private banks, insurers and utilities still require separate contact.
- [Death Notification Service](https://www.deathnotificationservice.co.uk/) — participating UK financial providers; check the current membership and privacy terms.
- [MoneyHelper: Finances after a partner dies](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/family-and-care/death-and-bereavement/dealing-with-finances-and-insurance-after-your-partner-dies.html) — bank accounts, insurance, benefits and household costs.
- [GOV.UK: Find lost pensions](https://www.gov.uk/find-pension-contact-details) — free contact-finding, not a pension balance search.
- [GOV.UK: Find a charity](https://www.gov.uk/find-charity-information) — confirm the legal name of an animal rescue/cattery or other charitable beneficiary.

**Suggested private handover checklist:** Location of the original will; who the executors are; named banks and ISA providers; pension/insurance providers; where house/title records live; a list of bills and the account that pays them; key contacts; document locations; instructions for lawful password-manager emergency access held separately. **Never put passwords, PINs, recovery codes or full bank numbers in a will or public file.**

## 3. DIY Probate — official route

- [GOV.UK: Apply for probate](https://www.gov.uk/applying-for-probate) — determine whether a grant is needed, value the estate and find the online application.
- [GOV.UK: How to apply yourself](https://www.gov.uk/applying-for-probate/apply-for-probate) — online/post options, costs and fee help.
- [GOV.UK: Original will rules](https://www.gov.uk/applying-for-probate/if-theres-a-will) — a photocopy is not normally accepted and the original becomes a public record.
- [GOV.UK: Inheritance Tax overview](https://www.gov.uk/inheritance-tax) — thresholds, relevant exemptions and reporting.
- [Citizens Advice: Financial affairs after a death](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/family/death-and-wills/dealing-with-the-financial-affairs-of-someone-who-has-died/) — straightforward administration and when advice is prudent.

**Cost caution:** An executor can often do probate personally; there is no universal requirement to hire the original will-writing firm. A simple estate may not need a grant at all. Check the government's current fees instead of repeating a static figure.

## 4. Build the Family Legacy — investments, gifts and family help

### ISA and first-home opportunities

- [GOV.UK: Lifetime ISA](https://www.gov.uk/lifetime-isa) — **open between 18 and 39**; eligible contributions up to **£4,000 a tax year until age 50**, plus a **25% government bonus**; first-home purchase conditions include at least 12 months since first payment, buying with a mortgage, a conveyancer and property price rules. The account is in the adult child's name, **not a family/joint ISA**.
- [MoneyHelper: Lifetime ISA guide](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/savings/types-of-savings/a-guide-to-lifetime-isas) — an independent, easier-to-read explanation.
- **A parent or grandparent of any age can gift money to an eligible adult child**, who can contribute from funds that belong to them. Account-provider payment rules vary; do not assume the parent can pay directly into a child's LISA. [HMRC ISA subscription rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/manage-isa-subscriptions-for-your-investors).
- [GOV.UK: Junior ISA](https://www.gov.uk/junior-individual-savings-accounts) — for under-18s, with **£9,000** annual contribution allowance in 2026/27. Anyone can contribute after the account is established, but the money belongs to the child and becomes available at 18.
- [GOV.UK: ISA overview](https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts) — personal (not joint) tax-efficient saving; 2026/27 overall adult allowance **£20,000**.
- [GOV.UK: Help to Buy ISA](https://www.gov.uk/help-to-buy-isa) — closed to new savers in 2019; existing savers can still contribute under scheme rules and claim bonus by November 2030.
- [HM Treasury Budget 2026: First Time Buyer ISA](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/budget-2026-document/budget-2026-html) — **announced for April 2029, not yet available**; planned 25% bonus, £4,000 annual contribution cap and no upper age restriction. Legislation and final terms must be checked closer to launch.
- [MoneyHelper: Home buying government schemes](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/homes/buying-a-home/government-schemes-for-first-time-home-buyers-and-existing-homeowners) — a free comparison of other routes, including shared ownership.

### Tax-aware family capital

- [GOV.UK: IHT gifts](https://www.gov.uk/inheritance-tax/gifts) — seven-year lifetime gift rule and exemptions; not a magic route to give away a home while continuing to live rent-free.
- [GOV.UK: Passing on the family home](https://www.gov.uk/inheritance-tax/passing-on-home) — residence nil-rate band and gift-with-reservation rules.
- [GOV.UK: Trusts and IHT](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes/trusts-and-inheritance-tax) — creation, ten-year and exit tax charges in relevant circumstances.
- [GOV.UK: Trust registration](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes/registering-a-trust) — registration, exemptions and tax obligations.
- [Companies House: Set up a limited company](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation) — administrative starting point when comparing a family investment company; not advice to form one.
- [GOV.UK: Check an investment firm](https://register.fca.org.uk/) — FCA register; verify permissions and beware scams before investing family savings.
- [MoneyHelper: Savings and investments](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/savings) — free educational entry point, not a promise of returns.
- [Law Society: Find a solicitor](https://solicitors.lawsociety.org.uk/) and [STEP: Find a trust practitioner](https://advisingfamilies.org/uk/find-a-tep/) — compare qualifications and independently check costs/insurance.

**Family loan reminder:** A repayable loan to help a relative's mortgage deposit must be truthfully disclosed to their mortgage lender. It is not an outright gift. A family trust or company is not a tax-free bank; get written advice before pooling money or making loans as a business.
