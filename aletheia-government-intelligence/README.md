**Why separate?** UK NHS/school/council/MOD/central-government procurement must be driven by actual notices, buyer requirements, CPV and regulatory/ethical checks; rice crop, weather and Asian FOB indicators remain part of Rice Intelligence.

**Business pathway:** identify actual buyer requirement → find authorized UK distributor/framework route → global producer Go Walkabout → specifications, conformity and landed-cost due diligence → transparent agency/sourcing agreement. Do **not** infer that finding a supplier means winning a public contract. Overseas connections provide research leads, never an exemption from tender or import rules.

**What is operational at v0.1:** source-backed Markdown instructions, seeded source catalogue, sector filters, local-only settings/report export and on-demand AI copy/open handoff (if client/browser permits). **What is NOT operational:** live tender indexing, autonomous web crawling, continuous monitoring, supplier verification, price quotes, bidding, sending contacts or paying. The chosen AI independently needs web tools for a current run. Do not claim live/device testing until carried out.

A 2027 GovNet whitepaper supplied by the project owner informed the buyer-sector map. Primary-source links in the source catalogue supersede the whitepaper's unverified claims. The public files include no private broker/customer/employer information.
