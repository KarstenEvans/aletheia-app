# Ageing War — pigmented rice and intestinal glucose utilization

[Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol) · [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md)

Status: PROPOSED, 27 November 2026.

intestinal complex I inhibition and glycolysis from metformin/berberine established mechanistically in 2026 (human metabolomics plus mouse genetics). Red rice bran proanthocyanidin-rich extracts inhibited digestive enzymes and enhanced glucose uptake in cell models, but there is no evidence here of identical intestinal complex I action in humans eating red rice. UKMRC9 trial found no significant lactate difference. Distinguish enzyme inhibition, luminal glucose absorption, epithelial glucose utilization, and microbial fermentation. Proposed follow-up: matched digestible starch, intact vs polished bran, purified fractions vs whole cooked grain; measure epithelial oxygen consumption/complex I, labelled glucose flux to lactate, Lac-Phe, GDF15, SCFAs and inflammatory markers; appropriate controls and human validation. https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-026-01530-y ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27285791/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32739823/

This is a research lead, not dietary treatment advice. Do not infer a complex-I effect from lower postprandial glucose alone.
