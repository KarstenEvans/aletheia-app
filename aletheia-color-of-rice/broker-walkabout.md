## Import controls and quality gates
- Dehusked, unpolished pigmented bran; no inedible hull. Sample each lot for iAs, Cd, Pb, pesticide residues, relevant mycotoxins and microbiological/quality indicators; use accredited lab scope and representative sampling. Retain farm/irrigation provenance and batch traceability. Bengal Delta groundwater contamination is a known risk, not a blanket rejection of country origins. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9288370/
- 2026/27 UK monitoring plan includes Thailand/Vietnam rice for heavy metals. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-national-monitoring-plan-sampling-priorities-for-imported-foods/uk-national-monitoring-plan-nmp-sampling-priorities-for-imported-foods-20262027
