## Evidence register

| Source | Finding | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Se et al. 2016](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/8/5/308) | 12 healthy adults, crossover: UKMRC9 GI 46 ±7.7, insulin index 51 ±5.3; basmati GI 50 ±5.8, II 52 ±5.3; Thai red GI 55 ±8.6; UKMRC10 GI 59 ±8.8; UKMRC11 GI 63 ±8.6; jasmine II 76 ±7.1. UKMRC9 insulin response significantly lower than jasmine, glucose comparison borderline. | Acute, specific samples, not T2D remission. |
| [IRRI 20 Apr 2026](https://ricetoday.irri.org/not-all-whole-grain-rice-is-low-glycemic-what-new-research-reveals/), [Pautong et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.carbpol.2026.125236) | 384 wholegrain accessions; fine starch structure predicts lab GI better than colour; considerable overlap. | In-vitro GI, not clinical testing of 384 varieties. |
| [Nomatech reported RICH trial](https://www.nomatech.com.my/clinical-study-on-red-pigmented-rice-ukmrc-9/) | 101 adults with T2D, 24-week randomised trial; supplier-hosted summary reports improvements in glycaemic markers and insulin resistance. | Full independent publication, registration and between-group estimates not verified here. No commercial claims. |
| [Callcott et al. 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31729520/) and [second study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31750484/) | Acute changes in selected inflammatory markers in healthy/obese adults. | Short-term biomarker endpoints. |
| [Mekhora et al. 2026](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/fo/d5fo04351d) | 24 older adults, crossover; black rice IL-6 fell from baseline, several other endpoints not significantly improved. | Small, short. Not applicable to all black rice. |
| [2023 review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13668-023-00496-7) | 17 RCTs; pooled chronic fasting glucose −1.60 mg/dL (~−0.09 mmol/L). | Small clinical magnitude; heterogeneous studies. |
| [2025 review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41110-025-00332-8) | 16 RCTs; no significant chronic fasting glucose benefit. | Abstract, preserve disagreement. |
| [Riceberry human trial](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-nutrition/article/riceberry-rice-oryza-sativa-l-slows-gastric-emptying-and-improves-the-postprandial-glycaemic-response/C11F4A94EDEE25D719BF2E3F942B7644) | Six healthy men; lower postprandial glucose and slower gastric emptying; insulin unchanged. | Small; delayed gastric emptying may matter for tolerance. |
| [Pigment chemistry](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cche.10861) | Red rice often rich in proanthocyanidins; purple/black often anthocyanins. | Chemistry not clinical benefit. |
| [High amylose white rice](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/7/7/5362) | 18-person human study, lower glucose and insulin response versus comparator. | Colour is not necessary for lower glycaemia. |
| [2026 mouse study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41543331/) | Engineered rice starch–anthocyanin complexes altered SCFAs and gut microbiota in T2D mice. | Not ordinary cooked rice or human evidence. C15/LPS effects unknown. |
| [EFSA arsenic exposure](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2021.6380) | Retained bran can increase inorganic arsenic exposure. | Ask for lot data rather than infer from colour. |

## Bounded Walkabout: three returns
1. **Rice passport** — compare cultivar, human GI/II vs lab estimate, starch, pigment, contaminants, harvest and packaging. Source: IRRI 2026, Se 2016. Challenge: lot-to-study equivalence and assay costs. Test: request documents and samples from Nomatech, Excel and Urmatt; do not promise performance.
2. **Gut fermentation / ageing research** — resistant starch and pigment–starch interaction are candidates for studying microbiome metabolism, acetate, propionate and butyrate. Source: 2026 mouse complex study above. Challenge: animal model and engineered ingredient, not ordinary rice; human C15, endotoxin and sustained inflammation effects remain unknown. Test: find human dietary intervention studies, not self-medication.
3. **Speciality tasting flight** — use the cultural colour association as a memorable entry, not a celebrity endorsement. [Purple Rain (1984)](https://discography.prince.com/albums/purple-rain), [Money Don’t Matter 2 Night (1991)](https://discography.prince.com/albums/diamonds-and-pearls), [The Color of Money (1986)](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/moviedetails/57292) are distinct works. Original concept: “Purple Grain” as an editorial pun only, not adopted brand; “Change the grain. Not necessarily the meal.” Challenge: name clearance, copyright, food claims and premium price. Test: 3-variety sample pack or domestic sample bags; assess taste, texture, cost, repeat purchase, not medical outcomes. No celebrity imagery or lyrics. No rights clearance performed.

## Pilot proposal
Start with red UKMRC9 if obtainable, Thai purple Riceberry and non-glutinous black rice; keep a white basmati comparator. Order samples only after quotes and compliance checks. Build a documented delivered cost per kg; evaluate sample size, packaging, storage, losses, market acceptance and repeat purchase before a container purchase. For imports, verify customs, food business registration, supplier traceability, GB contaminant requirements and claims rules with competent advice. Do not market as a diabetes treatment or imply guaranteed glucose/inflammation reductions. [GOV.UK food claims](https://www.gov.uk/food-labelling-and-packaging/nutrition-health-claims-and-supplement-labelling), [ASA CAP section 15](https://www.asa.org.uk/type/non_broadcast/code_section/15.html).

## v0.2 evidence extension — 27 November 2026
- **UKMRC9:** 19.8% amylose vs 23.0% jasmine, 4.96% fibre vs 0.24%; lower insulin index; earlier polishing removed favourable properties. No significant postprandial lactate difference across six rice samples. RS2/RS3 and individual pigments not quantified in that study. https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/8/5/308
- **Pigments:** a 2024 study of whole grain reported upper observed total anthocyanin values 239, 3.9 and 2.98 mg/100 g in black, red and non-pigmented samples respectively; PAC upper values 14, 204 and 0.44 mg catechin equivalents/100 g respectively. Assay equivalents differ; no cross-study inference or health threshold. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11039891/
- **Starch:** RS2 native granules, RS3 retrograded starch, RS1 physical inaccessibility, RS5 amylose–lipid complexes. IRRI screened total RS, digestible carbohydrate and fine chain lengths, not specifically RS2 as the explanation. Measure cooked grain. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42002323/ ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10800921/
- **Human inflammation:** Callcott (2019), 24 healthy participants, Purple IL-6 −11.7% at 1 h; Yunlu29 −14.1% at 2 h. Short-term biomarkers, not chronic clinical efficacy. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31729520/ ; black rice 2026 trial https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41528201/
- **Research hypothesis for the Ageing War (not verified):** intestinal complex I inhibition and glycolysis from metformin/berberine established mechanistically in 2026 (human metabolomics plus mouse genetics). Red rice bran proanthocyanidin-rich extracts inhibited digestive enzymes and enhanced glucose uptake in cell models, but there is no evidence here of identical intestinal complex I action in humans eating red rice. UKMRC9 trial found no significant lactate difference. Distinguish enzyme inhibition, luminal glucose absorption, epithelial glucose utilization, and microbial fermentation. Proposed follow-up: matched digestible starch, intact vs polished bran, purified fractions vs whole cooked grain; measure epithelial oxygen consumption/complex I, labelled glucose flux to lactate, Lac-Phe, GDF15, SCFAs and inflammatory markers; appropriate controls and human validation. https://www.nature.com/articles/s42255-026-01530-y ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27285791/ ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32739823/
- **Commercial:** shortlist dual-trait candidates, request representative lot composition and food safety certificates. No disease treatment claims. No supplier contacted; no live deployment.
