What this prototype does, and doesn't do

Evidence first

Prices are labelled by quotation date, unit, grade and trade basis. Futures are not substituted for Cambodian or Thai physical fragrant rice. Historical and discontinued feeds must not be presented as current.

Supplier names are research leads only, not guaranteed availability, legitimacy, quality or current offers. Always perform appropriate counterparty checks.

Private trading remains private

No WhatsApp, WeChat, email, account or company information is fetched by this standalone file. Do not paste contracts, credit limits or personal data into an unapproved public AI. No purchase, sale, message or alert scheduling is automated.

Optional Worker endpoint requires an independently hosted, authorised service with server-side credentials and protections.
