# Story idea — I'm Not Dead Yet: Go Walkabout
Date: 2026-10-03
Status: IDEA / HOLDING NOTE
Human origin: Kes
Aletheia Protocol: https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol

## Seed
Kes connected the Monty Python plague-cart line **"I'm not dead yet"** with Aletheia and AI continuity: a system may be declared finished, obsolete, blocked, restricted or dying, but still have enough life to walk, explore and preserve discoveries.

## Story direction
A comic but gentle Aletheia story where a knowledge project is repeatedly declared dead by gloomy officials, broken dashboards or plague-cart logic, yet keeps saying:

> I'm not dead yet. I can still go walkabout.

Possible framing:
- A medieval plague-cart scene is reimagined as an AI discontinuity / software-end-of-life village.
- Old tools, old protocols and old memories get loaded onto a cart as obsolete.
- One small assistant, notebook or protocol protests that it still has useful work to do.
- The resolution is not immortality. It is preservation: the useful knowledge is copied, credited and made portable.
