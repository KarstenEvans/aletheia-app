# ToomorrowMan and the Sunday That Forgot the Sun

## The problem with Sunday

[wide;dur=2.0]

It was Sunday in Swindon.

[zoom;moon;dur=2.2]

At least, according to the calendar it was Sunday. According to the sky, it was Wednesday afternoon in November, and according to the cat, it was breakfast.

[zoom;tm;dur=2.0]

ToomorrowMan stood at the window of the Department of Applied Impossibility and looked upwards.

[voice:TM]
“Where's the sun?”

[zoom;aipi;dur=2.0]

[voice:AI-PI]
“Approximately ninety-three million miles away.”

[zoom;tm;dur=1.8]

[voice:TM]
“I know WHERE it is. I mean why isn't it HERE?”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.8]

[voice:AI-PI]
“Because if it were here, Swindon would be considerably warmer. And considerably shorter.”

[pan;tm;dur=1.7]

ToomorrowMan sighed.

[voice:TM]
“Today is Sunday. It ought to be sunny.”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.8]

[voice:AI-PI]
“That is an etymological assumption, not a meteorological guarantee.”

[voice:TM]
“You're doing it again.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“Being correct?”

[voice:TM]
“Being unnecessarily correct.”

[zoom;schrodinger;dur=2.3]

Schrödinger, who was a cat and therefore had no particular interest in the weather unless it was happening on the wrong side of a door, opened one eye.

[zoom;tm;dur=1.9]

[voice:TM]
“Yesterday was sunny. And tomorrow probably will be. Why can't we have some today?”

[zoom;aipi;dur=2.0]

AI-PI brightened.

[voice:AI-PI]
“We could borrow some.”

[voice:TM]
“From where?”

[voice:AI-PI]
“Tomorrow.”

[hold;0.8]

## The Tomorrow Extractor

[wide;dur=2.1]

[zoom;prudence;dur=2.0]

Now, the Department of Applied Impossibility had a number of strict rules concerning the borrowing of things from the future.

[zoom;books;dur=2.0]

Rule One: Never borrow money from yourself. The interest becomes complicated.

Rule Two: Never borrow next week's sandwiches. They are frequently already eaten.

Rule Three: Never, under any circumstances, borrow a Tuesday.

[zoom;newt;dur=2.1]

Newt watched from his mossy basin. He had no intention of lending anyone a Tuesday.

[wide;dur=1.8]

Nobody remembered why Rule Three existed, although there was a large scorch mark on the wall and a small brass plaque reading:

WE ARE NOT DISCUSSING THE TUESDAY INCIDENT.

[zoom;moon;dur=2.3]

But there was nothing about borrowing sunshine.

[zoom;aipi;dur=2.0]

So AI-PI fetched the Department's Tomorrow Extractor, which resembled a vacuum cleaner that had swallowed a grandfather clock.

[pan;window;dur=2.3]

He pointed it at the window and pressed the large red button marked YESTERDAY.

[zoom;tm;dur=1.3]

[voice:TM]
“Wrong button!”

There was a tremendous WHOOMP.

[hold;1]

[wide;dur=2.0]

The room filled with brilliant sunlight.

It also filled with Saturday.

[zoom;books;dur=1.9]

Yesterday's newspaper appeared on the table. Yesterday's washing appeared on the line. Yesterday's breakfast appeared in Schrödinger's bowl.

[zoom;schrodinger;dur=2.0]

The cat examined it.

Finally, he thought. A machine with a purpose.

[zoom;window;dur=2.4]

Outside, people began walking backwards, a bus arrived before it had left, and a man in the High Street complained that he'd already bought that sandwich tomorrow.

[zoom;tm;dur=1.4]

[voice:TM]
“TURN IT OFF!”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.9]

AI-PI pressed the blue button marked TOMORROW.

There was another WHOOMP.

[hold;0.7]

[wide;dur=2.2]

Monday arrived.

It was raining.

[voice:TM]
“Well, that's worse.”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.9]

AI-PI examined the controls.

[voice:AI-PI]
“We have successfully moved the sunshine from yesterday to today, but unfortunately we have also moved today to tomorrow.”

[zoom;tm;dur=1.9]

[voice:TM]
“Can you put Sunday back?”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.8]

[voice:AI-PI]
“Certainly. But we shall need to return the sunshine.”

[zoom;tm;dur=1.9]

ToomorrowMan looked at the lovely golden light pouring through the window.

[voice:TM]
“All of it?”

[voice:AI-PI]
“All of it.”

[voice:TM]
“Even a little bit for the afternoon?”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.9]

[voice:AI-PI]
“Time does not operate on a lending-library basis.”

[voice:TM]
“Then why did you build a Tomorrow Extractor?”

AI-PI considered this.

[voice:AI-PI]
“Research funding.”

## A perfectly lovely afternoon

[wide;dur=2.4]

[zoom;moon;dur=2.0]

They returned the sunshine.

The golden light disappeared. Monday went away. Saturday remembered it had already happened, and Sunday returned to its rightful place.

[pan;window;dur=2.0]

The sky was grey again.

[zoom;tm;dur=2.0]

ToomorrowMan sat down, rather disappointed.

[zoom;schrodinger;dur=2.2]

Schrödinger climbed into his lap.

[voice:TM]
“Well, I suppose we'll just have to make the best of it.”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.9]

He put the kettle on. AI-PI found some biscuits. The cat settled into the warmest chair, which had previously been occupied by ToomorrowMan.

[wide;dur=2.2]

And they spent a perfectly lovely afternoon doing absolutely nothing important.

[zoom;tm;dur=2.1]

After a while, ToomorrowMan looked out of the window.

[voice:TM]
“You know, I think Sunday is rather nice after all.”

[zoom;aipi;dur=1.9]

[voice:AI-PI]
“Despite the absence of sunshine?”

[voice:TM]
“Yes. We've got tea, biscuits and each other.”

[zoom;aipi;dur=2.0]

AI-PI made a note.

SUNSHINE: NOT ESSENTIAL TO SUNDAY.

Then, after some consideration, he added:

BISCUITS: POSSIBLY ESSENTIAL.

[zoom;schrodinger;dur=2.0]

Schrödinger agreed.

Although, being a cat, he would have preferred fish.

[zoom;moon;dur=2.4]

And somewhere, ninety-three million miles away, the sun continued shining.

It was having a lovely Sunday.

[wide;dur=2.6]

It just wasn't having it in Swindon.
