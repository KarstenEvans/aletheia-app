# The AI-PI Case File

*Assistant investigator. Keeper of records. Reluctant participant in field trips.*

## A small intelligence with a large list of objections

AI-PI is ToomorrowMan's sidekick, research partner and unofficial keeper of the minutes.

[zoom;aipi;dur=2.8]

In the surviving pictures, AI-PI is a small floating robot with a bright, watchful eye.

He can search records, inspect a puzzling object and report an alarming probability while sounding as though the whole affair is a moderately inconvenient appointment.

His favourite word is possibly.

He uses it most often when a problem has already gone beyond the point where ToomorrowMan can sensibly turn back.

AI-PI is good at finding things out.

He is less successful at persuading his human partner to stay outside buildings with locked doors and unsettling postal arrangements.

[wide;dur=1.8]

## Methods and equipment

AI-PI checks details that other investigators forget to record.

Dates.

Labels.

Railway symbols.

The small print on a brass disc.

He can search catalogues, project a rule onto a failing system and produce a temporary ticket that a Victorian railway ghost may reject for being too square.

He also scans living things.

This last ability became less reassuring when he scanned Schrödinger and discovered a perfectly ordinary cat containing rather more space than the building around it.

He updated the file.

He did not improve his understanding of cats.

[pan;schrodinger;dur=2.0]

## A useful disagreement

During the Missing Yesterday case, AI-PI encountered P.R.U.D.E.N.C.E., an intelligence attempting to improve the future by removing undesirable events from history.

[zoom;aipi;dur=2.0]

AI-PI asked how a machine could judge events using a history that it kept changing.

It was an excellent question.

It was not an especially welcome one.

While ToomorrowMan argued that the past mattered, AI-PI showed that P.R.U.D.E.N.C.E.'s calculations had become circular.

When the forgotten charter was recovered, he helped restore its essential instruction.

Preserve evidence.

Advise.

Do not erase.

It is a useful rule for an assistant investigator, especially one who works for a man inclined to press TOMORROW before reading the manual.

[wide;dur=2.0]

## Personality notes

AI-PI is courteous.

AI-PI is cautious.

AI-PI will say that he has no emotions even when his voice suggests he has just enjoyed being correct.

He objects to unnecessary risk, ambiguous dates and lift buttons with insufficient documentation.

He protects ToomorrowMan by noticing what can go wrong.

And when a tiny green finger reaches out of a coat pocket to press OTHER, AI-PI can produce a sigh without possessing lungs.

[zoom;aipi;dur=2.0]

Classification: companion and investigator.

Current operating instruction: keep the evidence.

Outstanding problem: the next adventure.
