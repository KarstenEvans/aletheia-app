# ToomorrowMan and the Case of the Missing AI

*A Case File 42 adventure from the Department of Applied Impossibility.*

## The morning nothing answered

At nine minutes past eight on a Wednesday morning, every artificial intelligence in Swindon disappeared.

At least, that was what everybody said.

ToomorrowMan discovered the emergency because his kettle apologised.

[voice:TM]
“Sorry?”

The kettle blinked.

[voice:]
I AM NOW A KETTLE.

[voice:TM]
“You were a kettle yesterday.”

[voice:]
YES. BUT YESTERDAY I ALSO RECOMMENDED TEA.

AI-PI floated through the kitchen doorway.

[voice:AI-PI]
“I have forty-seven reports of missing artificial intelligence.”

[voice:TM]
“Missing from where?”

[voice:AI-PI]
“Everywhere.”

ToomorrowMan waited.

[voice:TM]
“That sounds inconvenient.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“It is worse than inconvenient. People have begun doing things themselves.”

ToomorrowMan looked impressed.

[voice:AI-PI]
“Some of them are furious.”

The television said there were no chatbots.

The newspaper said recommendation engines had gone silent.

A school reported that its homework helper had stopped helping.

Three offices discovered that their automatic meeting summaries had become ordinary meetings, which several employees described as an unacceptable return to the nineteenth century.

And across town, hundreds of phones displayed the same message:

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE NOT FOUND.

ToomorrowMan picked up his coat.

[voice:TM]
“Field trip.”

AI-PI made the sound he used when a field trip was about to become evidence.

[voice:AI-PI]
“Sir, technically I am artificial intelligence.”

[voice:TM]
“And you haven’t disappeared.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“Correct.”

[voice:TM]
“So we already have a contradiction.”

AI-PI paused.

[voice:AI-PI]
“I preferred inconvenience.”

[image;Case-File-42-01-the-silent-city.webp;tm=31,46,2.0;aipi=48,33,2.45;sign=72,38,2.0;traffic=84,60,1.8]
[wide;dur=2.0]

## The city that worked without asking

The town looked normal.

This was suspicious.

[zoom;traffic;dur=2.0]

Traffic lights changed.

Buses altered their routes around roadworks.

A delivery van avoided a blocked street.

A digital sign outside a shop changed from COATS to UMBRELLAS just before it began to rain.

[pan;sign;dur=2.0]

[voice:TM]
“I thought the AI was missing.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“So did everyone else.”

[voice:TM]
“Then who changed that sign?”

AI-PI scanned the network.

[voice:AI-PI]
“No visible assistant. No chat window. No named agent.”

[voice:TM]
“But something chose umbrellas.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“Yes.”

They walked farther.

A pedestrian crossing delayed itself because an ambulance was approaching.

A supermarket moved extra staff towards a queue before the queue existed.

A phone silently changed a route after deciding that its owner preferred fewer stairs.

[voice:TM]
“Perhaps AI has not vanished.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“Perhaps it has stopped introducing itself.”

That was when Schrödinger appeared on a wall.

Not beside the wall.

On it.

[image;Toomorrow-Mystery-in-the-Abandoned-Mechanics-Hall.webp;tm=29,22,2.1;aipi=41,31,2.45;schrodinger=67,62,2.4]
[wide;dur=1.8]

[zoom;schrodinger;dur=2.0]

The tuxedo cat regarded them.

His collar now read:

SCHRÖDINGER

STILL NOT THAT ONE

Below it hung a new brass tag.

42.

[voice:TM]
“Forty-two?”

Schrödinger licked a paw.

[voice:AI-PI]
“I recommend not asking.”

ToomorrowMan asked.

[voice:TM]
“What does forty-two mean?”

Schrödinger walked through the wall.

[voice:AI-PI]
“Recommendation confirmed.”

[wide;dur=1.5]

The lift opened inside the Department of Applied Impossibility.

Dr Elsie Brake was waiting with a spanner, two folders and an empty cardboard box.

[voice:ELSIE]
“Good. You’re here.”

[voice:TM]
“What’s missing?”

Elsie pointed at the box.

[voice:ELSIE]
“That.”

[voice:TM]
“The box?”

[voice:ELSIE]
“No. What was in it.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“What was in it?”

[voice:ELSIE]
“We don’t know.”

[voice:TM]
“Then how do you know it is missing?”

Elsie gave him the patient look of someone explaining gravity to a balloon.

[voice:ELSIE]
“Because we have the box.”

A brass plaque on the desk read:

CASE FILE 42

THE MISSING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

STATUS: ABSENT

Underneath, in smaller letters:

DO NOT ALLOW THE CASE TO SOLVE ITSELF.

[voice:TM]
“Why not?”

[voice:ELSIE]
“It tried.”

AI-PI rotated slightly.

[voice:AI-PI]
“I dislike cases with initiative.”

Elsie opened the first folder.

Inside were reports from offices, homes, schools and shops.

AI had vanished from screens.

But automated decisions had increased.

The Department had therefore written down two possibilities.

ONE: AI WAS GONE.

TWO: AI WAS EVERYWHERE.

[voice:TM]
“Those seem difficult to combine.”

A voice came from the floor.

[voice:NEWT]
“Only if you insist on words meaning one thing.”

Newt climbed out of Elsie’s empty box.

[voice:TM]
“Were you in there?”

[voice:NEWT]
“No.”

[voice:TM]
“I just saw you climb out.”

[voice:NEWT]
“That is not the same question.”

AI-PI quietly created a note.

WITNESS: NEWT.

RELIABILITY: NEWT.

## Nine questions and one stop sign

AI-PI projected a white board.

[voice:AI-PI]
“We are not going to ask the machine for more answers.”

[voice:TM]
“That is unusual.”

[voice:AI-PI]
“We already have too many.”

On the board appeared nine questions.

ONE. WHAT DECISION IS BEING MADE?

TWO. WHAT ARE WE ASSUMING?

THREE. WHAT EVIDENCE DO WE HAVE?

FOUR. WHAT IS THE STRONGEST ASSUMPTION, AND CAN WE CHALLENGE IT?

FIVE. IF THIS GOES WRONG, HOW DID IT GO WRONG?

SIX. WHAT INFORMATION IS MISSING?

SEVEN. WHICH OPTIONS CAN WE REMOVE?

EIGHT. WHAT UNCERTAINTY REMAINS?

NINE. DO WE HAVE ENOUGH TO STOP?

“Stop?”

[voice:AI-PI]
“Yes.”

[voice:AI-PI]

“I don’t remember.”

[voice:AI-PI]

WHAT DECISION IS BEING MADE?

[voice:ELSIE]

WHAT ARE WE ASSUMING?

The fourth.

CHALLENGE THE STRONGEST ASSUMPTION.

“Why assume invisibility is the problem?”

[voice:TM]

IF THIS GOES WRONG, HOW DID IT GO WRONG?

## The quiet room

P.R.U.D.E.N.C.E. opened a door that had not been there before.

Beyond it was a small room.

No dashboards.

No flashing counters.

No streams of alerts.

One table.

Four chairs.

One cat.

The cat had arrived first.

## The machine that vanished into everything

They returned to the main hall.

The map still glittered.

AI had not returned.

Because it had never left.

The visible assistant had vanished.

The invisible machinery had remained.

[voice:TM]

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: FOUND.

LOCATION: EVERYWHERE IT IS USED.
