## Creative brief
Format: 16:9 landscape, ~4–7 minutes depending on the final reading pace. Gentle read-aloud for children and others preferring low-intensity media. Soft stationary illustrations; only optional slow dissolves. No rapid edits, music requirement, flicker, forced motion or alarming noises. Always offer captions and text-first alternative.
Working video title: **Pip and the Maple's Secret | A Gentle Aletheia Story**
Subtitle: **Follow the Little AI's Treasure/Memory Map, Kids!** is for the *Forgetful AI* story family, not forced as this story's title. Cross-link the series discreetly.

## Six original visual prompt briefs (NOT GENERATED)
1. Wide quiet maple-tree clearing in late afternoon; grown door softly luminous, child carrying an abstract question-shaped key; friendly tiny bee with green checked vest. Watercolour picture-book style, accessible contrast, no branded characters.
2. Golden maple leaf, calm glow, child quietly looking; whimsical but not frightening or overly saturated.
3. Explorer bee Bramble with subtle checked vest, introducing a tiny butterfly with keyhole-wing markings; maintain character continuity.
4. Close-up of leaf in an unnamed warm iridescent colour; visualise as soft pastel overlay, **never strobe/flash**.
5. Bark with a carefully preserved childlike sketch of a keywing butterfly; evidence of an earlier drawing, gentle nostalgia without fear.
6. Warm hollow with recovered folded drawing, smiling child, Pip hovering and Bramble nearby; low movement ending with closing quotation.
All prompts are prospective and subject to rights/design review.
