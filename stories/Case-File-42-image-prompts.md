## Canon lock
Use the existing published images and bios as the visual reference for ToomorrowMan, AI-PI and Schrödinger before generating any new artwork:
- ToomorrowMan: `Toomorrowman02.webp`
- AI-PI: `Newt-Presses-Other.webp` and existing story images
- Schrödinger: `Toomorrow-Mystery-in-the-Abandoned-Mechanics-Hall.webp` and `bio-Schrodinger.md`
- Elsie / Prudence: use their current bios for role and behaviour; do not redesign established characters merely because an image generator varies.
- Overall look: cinematic illustrated storybook realism, warm brass/gold against deep blue, Swindon/railway/Department visual language, child-friendly mystery rather than horror.
- Keep character face, hair, clothing, robot proportions and cat markings consistent with the existing references.

## 1 — Case-File-42-01-the-silent-city.webp
Wide cinematic Swindon street in early morning. ToomorrowMan and small floating AI-PI investigate a city where visible AI interfaces have gone silent but ordinary systems still quietly act: traffic lights adapt, a bus route display changes, an umbrella shop sign updates before rain. Deep navy and warm gold lighting. The mystery is subtle: technology works, but nobody can see who or what made the choices. Hotspot-friendly composition with ToomorrowMan left, AI-PI centre-left, digital sign right, traffic far right.

## 2 — Case-File-42-02-department-missing-desk.webp
Inside the Department of Applied Impossibility, an impossible Victorian archive hidden behind Swindon's Mechanics' Institution. Dr Elsie Brake stands beside a desk holding an empty cardboard evidence box and a brass case plaque marked only with a large 42 symbol, not long readable text. ToomorrowMan and AI-PI face her. Brass cabinets, misty balconies and railway bureaucracy. Leave clear visual regions for Elsie, the empty box, ToomorrowMan and AI-PI.

## 3 — Case-File-42-03-prudence-everywhere.webp
The Department's central room with P.R.U.D.E.N.C.E. represented in the established calm institutional style, while a huge map/grid reveals tiny golden lights throughout roads, hospitals, phones, shops, factories, calendars and buildings. The revelation is that AI has vanished from the interface but spread into ordinary infrastructure. ToomorrowMan, AI-PI and Elsie watch the map. No dystopian villain imagery.

## 4 — Case-File-42-04-nine-questions.webp
AI-PI projects a clean evidence board with nine simple numbered symbols or short headings, not dense generated text. ToomorrowMan studies it. Schrödinger, the established tuxedo Dragonfold cat with exact black-and-white markings, sits on the edge of the board with a small brass 42 tag. A red stop-sign symbol waits beneath the sequence. Visual theme: investigation, evidence and decision-making.

## 5 — Case-File-42-05-quiet-room.webp
A surprisingly calm room inside the impossible Department. No dashboards or alert walls. One wooden table, four chairs, one simple card at the centre, ToomorrowMan, AI-PI, Elsie, and Schrödinger already occupying the best chair. Soft warm light. The room embodies cognitive quiet: only what changed, what matters, and what needs attention.

## 6 — Case-File-42-06-vanished-into-everything.webp
Final cinematic reveal. ToomorrowMan and AI-PI stand overlooking a modern city merged with the impossible Department. Fine golden circuitry and constellation-like points pass almost invisibly through traffic systems, buildings, phones and signs, showing intelligence embedded in everything rather than a single robot or screen. Schrödinger sits nearby with a small 42 tag. Hopeful, curious ending, not ominous. Strong empty space for the story's final captions.

## Article / social hero artwork
Title concept: “Case File 42: The Artificial Intelligence That Vanished Into Everything — A Sif and AI-PI Investigation.”
Sif is the established kind Nordic-inspired Aletheia archaeologist/detective from the Cabinet artwork, accompanied by the established small floating AI-PI. They investigate an empty glowing outline where a visible AI interface used to be. Around it, ordinary systems quietly make choices: traffic, routing, recommendations, calendars and doors. Faint gold data trails dissolve into the city itself. Schrödinger may sit in the foreground with an understated 42 tag. Deep navy, brass and gold, intelligent mystery, elegant rather than cyberpunk. No text rendered into the artwork; page HTML supplies the title.
