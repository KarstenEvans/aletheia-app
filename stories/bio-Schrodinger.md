# Schrödinger: A Cat of Unreasonable Dimensions

*Species: Dragonfold. Current form: tuxedo cat.*

## Not that one

[zoom;schrodinger;dur=2.8]

Schrödinger is a black-and-white cat with the manners of a cat, the patience of a cat, and a collar bearing a name that comes with a warning.

NOT THAT ONE.

After the Missing Yesterday affair, the tag was revised.

STILL NOT THAT ONE.

The difference may be important to someone.

Schrödinger has declined to explain it.

ToomorrowMan first met him outside an impossible lift, on a floor that should not have existed.

The cat yawned.

AI-PI scanned him.

External mass: four point six kilograms.

Internal volume: disputed.

The scan implied a space considerably larger than any reasonable cat, railway carriage or building.

Schrödinger began washing a paw.

[wide;dur=2.0]

## What is a Dragonfold?

Some dragons become cats by folding their impossible size inward.

They can live at ordinary cat scale, protect a household or quietly shadow a child, and spend years giving every impression that the most urgent matter in the universe is an empty food bowl.

Their folded form is smaller on the outside.

What happens on the inside is not recommended for measurement.

When their time as cats is complete, a Dragonfold may unfold and return to the stars.

None of this prevents the creature from stealing a favourite chair.

In fact, it may explain the confidence with which the theft is undertaken.

[zoom;schrodinger;dur=2.1]

## The cat who defeated certainty

Schrödinger once walked through a wall.

Later he appeared on a train before his travelling companions.

Then he sat on the keyboard of P.R.U.D.E.N.C.E., a machine that insisted the future could be calculated into perfection.

For a moment the cat's outline doubled.

There were golden eyes.

There was the shadow of wings.

There was something vast curled around a room that no longer entirely agreed on how large it was.

The machine could not determine a single preferred outcome.

The others recovered the rule that it had tried to forget.

Schrödinger washed a paw.

[wide;dur=1.7]

## Operational guidance

Do not attempt to catalogue the interior.

Do not interpret indifference as inattention.

Do not assume the cat is absent simply because he has entered the wall.

And do not press him for an explanation.

Schrödinger is, after all, a cat.
