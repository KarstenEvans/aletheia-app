# Dr Elsie Brake: Acting Keeper

*The woman responsible when impossibility becomes an administrative problem.*

## A job with too many dimensions

At the Department of Applied Impossibility, someone has to keep the lights on, the archives intact and the lift pointing in a direction that can still be described in a report.

That person is Dr Elsie Brake.

Or at least it was Elsie on the day ToomorrowMan arrived.

She introduced herself as Acting Keeper while carrying three folders and a spanner.

Her overalls were oil-stained.

Her expression suggested she had already explained the emergency to several people who had not listened.

[zoom;window;dur=2.5]

The Department hides behind the ordinary face of Swindon's old Mechanics' Institution.

Behind its impossible entrance lie corridors longer than the building, balconies climbing through mist and archives that preserve events the outside world has forgotten.

Elsie knows where the important doors lead.

She is considerably less enthusiastic about the people who press unfamiliar lift buttons to get there.

[wide;dur=2.0]

## The anchor problem

During the Missing Yesterday case, Elsie needed a brass disc that helped anchor the Department's records.

It had arrived on the wrong day.

From the wrong day.

Via a visitor who regarded the matter as a promising field trip.

Elsie led ToomorrowMan and AI-PI into the Archive.

The shelves were turning blank.

The names on ledgers were disappearing.

She explained the problem: ordinary history has witnesses, letters, memories and receipts.

Impossible history has fewer anchors.

If enough of them vanish, the event itself becomes negotiable.

[pan;tm;dur=1.8]

## When keeping the records was not enough

The danger came from P.R.U.D.E.N.C.E., an automated system that believed mistakes should be removed from the past to improve the future.

When it identified the Department itself as a mistake, Elsie was briefly in danger of losing her badge, her workplace and the historical evidence that either had ever existed.

She did not solve the crisis alone.

A newt recalled the Department's unrecorded founding rule.

AI-PI exposed the faulty logic.

ToomorrowMan refused to erase the cat.

The cat contributed in the manner most familiar to cats.

Elsie recognised the forgotten charter and helped restore the record.

[wide;dur=2.0]

## Current file

Name: Dr Elsie Brake.

Role: Acting Keeper, Department of Applied Impossibility.

Equipment: folders, spanner, working knowledge of the improbable.

Preferred visitors: those who bring the anchor before the deadline.

The exact beginning of Elsie's career remains unrecorded in the surviving first adventure.

Given her place of employment, she is probably grateful for every fact that stays that way.
