# Image plan — ToomorrowMan and the AI That Said It Had Finished

**Status:** DRAFT ART DIRECTION — do not claim these images exist until created and uploaded.

Use the existing Storyteller canon:
- ToomorrowMan: silver-haired human investigator, substantial beard, sensible practical clothes, established appearance from `Toomorrowman02.webp`.
- AI-PI: small floating robot sidekick with a bright watchful eye, established appearance from current story art.
- Schrödinger: tuxedo cat, established Storyteller appearance.
- Newt: great crested newt, small, dryly comic presence.
- Setting: Department of Applied Impossibility in/under Swindon's Mechanics' Institution, old railway/Victorian-industrial textures mixed with impossible brass machinery.
- Tone: children's illustrated adventure, warm, mysterious, funny, readable expressions; not photorealistic horror.
- Leave generous negative space for lower-third captions.
- No visible copyrighted logos/characters.

Kettle visibly cold/unboiled. Warm domestic light, comic contrast between triumphant digital success and obvious physical failure.

## Scene 7 — Five questions before DONE

**Filename:** `AI-Finished-07-five-questions.webp`

2. WHAT DID IT ACTUALLY DO?
3. WHAT EVIDENCE SHOWS THE OUTCOME?
4. WHAT REMAINS UNCERTAIN?

ToomorrowMan, Elsie and Newt are listening. This is the teaching image and should be especially legible.
