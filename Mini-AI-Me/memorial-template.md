# Aletheia Mini-AI-Me: a memory worth sharing

- [Aletheia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-protocol): consent, evidence, provenance, privacy, corrections, human approval.
- [Thalia Protocol](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol/blob/main/THALIA_PROTOCOL.md): respect, thoughtful original humour and the right to be serious.

> **Opening thought:** A life isn't a collection of database fields. If someone insists on being described as a spreadsheet, at least give them a column marked *unexpected brilliance*.

## Choose the right category

- **Remember a real person who has died** — personal tribute or public-history study, with sensitivity to relatives and permissions.
- **Celebrate a fictional character or finished series** — nostalgia and interpretation, **not** a death notice about actors.
- **Celebrate a living person** — appreciation or creative study only with appropriate consent, no implied endorsement.

## Template to fill out

**Title:** [A memory of… / A celebration of…]

**Category:** [Real-person memorial / fictional-world celebration / living-person appreciation]

**Who or what are we remembering?** [The subject and factual context.]

**One memorable moment:** [Your own recollection, explicitly marked as a recollection, not an invented quotation.]

**What made people smile?** [Original description, gentle anecdote or new humorous observation.]

**One quiet thing worth remembering:** [Compassion, curiosity, courage, creativity, kindness…]

**Find their actual work:** [Links to authorised recordings, reputable biographical sources, books or approved archives.]

**Supporting sources and dates checked:** [Independent sources where claims need verification; label uncertainty.]

**Permission/rights check:** [Who approved publication, if required? Is copyrighted content avoided?]

**Would you like a Mini-AI-Me educational profile?** [Optional: 28-question original guided questionnaire, never a claim to recreate the person.]

## Publication checklist

- [ ] No invented first-person memories or forged quotations.
- [ ] No deepfake/voice clone or unlicensed copyrighted film excerpts, photos or logos.
- [ ] No sensitive private health, relationship or family information without permission.
- [ ] No equating actor with character; no false obituaries.
- [ ] Distinguish documented facts, personal memories and fictional creative writing.
- [ ] Verify links and use plain-English titles for accessibility.
- [ ] Obtain human approval before any public posting or site changes.

This is a **downloadable Markdown worksheet only**. It does not submit material to RIP UK, GitHub Discussions or any public service. Any submission system would require separate design, moderation, permissions and owner approval.

## Resume

Open `Mini-AI-Me/README.md`, the current repository instructions and this template before adding any public contribution feature. Ensure both protocols remain cited in any generated Markdown.
