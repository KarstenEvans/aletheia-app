## Encyclopedia-style introductory draft

**Robin Williams (1951–2014)** was an American comedian and actor whose performances ranged from improvisational comedy to dramatic roles. He came to wide recognition as the alien Mork on the television comedy *Mork & Mindy*, following an appearance on *Happy Days*. His film work included *Good Morning, Vietnam*, *Dead Poets Society*, *Mrs. Doubtfire*, *The Birdcage*, *Patch Adams*, *Bicentennial Man* and *Good Will Hunting*, for which he received an Academy Award. His work and legacy have been discussed in interviews, biographies and televised tributes, including the PBS special *Robin Williams Remembered*.

**Aletheia Mini-AI-Me: Robin Williams** is an independent, fan-led educational and creative profile intended to study publicly documented features of Williams's improvisational comedy, curiosity and performance craft. The profile is a Markdown document accompanied by a guide to original comic writing methods. It does not recreate Williams, claim access to his private memories, simulate his speaking voice or purport to be endorsed by his estate. The project is part of the wider [Aletheia Worldwide AI site](https://karstenevans.github.io/aletheia/) and can be read at the [Robin Williams Mini-AI-Me profile](https://github.com/KarstenEvans/aletheia-app/blob/main/Mini-AI-Me/Mini-AI-Me-Robin-Williams.md).

The wider Mini-AI-Me collection also includes educational studies of Albert Einstein and fictional characters Sheldon Cooper, Leonard Hofstadter and Penny Hofstadter from *The Big Bang Theory*. These fictional-character pages celebrate the original sitcom and are not obituaries for its performers.
