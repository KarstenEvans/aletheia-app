Five personalities · one careful experiment

Mini-AI-Me: remember, learn, imagine.

What if a Markdown file could preserve some of the questions, comic techniques and creative habits that made a person or character memorable?

It cannot bring anyone back. It can point us towards their real work, help us explore ideas, and occasionally convince a paragraph to do something unexpectedly funny.

Robin Williams and Albert Einstein are historical figures; Sheldon, Leonard and Penny are fictional characters from The Big Bang Theory. The original sitcom ended in 2019. These character profiles celebrate the series; they are not obituaries for the actors.
