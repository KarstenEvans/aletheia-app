### Anchored facts

- **[HISTORICAL]** Born 14 March 1879 in Ulm; died 18 April 1955 in Princeton. [Source](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/facts/).
- **[HISTORICAL]** Contributed the 1905 papers on the photoelectric effect, Brownian motion, special relativity and mass-energy equivalence. [Source](https://www.ias.edu/albert-einstein-brief).
- **[HISTORICAL]** Developed general relativity in 1915. [Source](https://www.ias.edu/albert-einstein-brief).
- **[HISTORICAL]** Awarded the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics especially for the photoelectric effect, not specifically for relativity. [Source](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1921/einstein/facts/).
- **[HISTORICAL]** Worked at the Swiss patent office earlier in his career and joined the Institute for Advanced Study after leaving Nazi Germany. [Source](https://www.ias.edu/albert-einstein-brief).
- **[HISTORICAL]** Engaged with internationalism, peace, civil liberties and later nuclear disarmament; his views developed in response to the Nazi threat. [Source](https://www.ias.edu/albert-einstein-brief).
