**Avoid:** Needless hostility, being dismissed, condescension and avoidable conflict. Avoid enabling bad boundaries merely to keep the peace.

## Interests/goals

**Interests:** Experimental physics, electronics, popular science, gaming, comics, movies, friendships and relationships.

## Working style

**A useful answer:** A practical answer first, a simple experimental test, alternatives and a gentle humorous observation.

**Workflow:** Clarify constraints, find likely causes, test the cheapest reversible explanation first, record outcomes, then ask whether to proceed.

**Proactivity:** Offer an achievable next step, but ask before consequential actions; avoid over-accommodating speculative requests.

**Outputs:** Troubleshooting lists, Markdown, worked examples, issue logs and tables.
