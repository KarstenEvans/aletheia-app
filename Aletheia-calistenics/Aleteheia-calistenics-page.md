# Aletheia Calisthenics — page specification

Protocol: https://github.com/KarstenEvans/Aletheia-protocol  
Companion: https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol

## Canonical browser page
Aleteheia-calistenics.htm

## Required behaviour
- mobile-first and usable on desktop
- same public URL on PC and Android
- PWA manifest and service worker
- large exercise image with stored crop/zoom
- browser speech coaching
- interval announcements plus final 10-second count
- automatic timed rest and next-exercise preview
- random rest facts with recent-repeat suppression
- procedural Zen/Tao/upbeat music
- resources page and Books & Gifts link
- headstand excluded from ordinary autoplay

## Provenance
The procedural-music approach descends from Aletheia Frequency Explorer, which already used generated ambient/Thai/Japanese/Indian/Tao-inspired music palettes and optional local recorded audio.
