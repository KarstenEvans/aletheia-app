## Calisthenics with Cats

*Just a mat, a cat, and an uninvited inspector.*

You roll out the mat. Mitten opens one eye.

This is unfortunate. Until a moment ago, Mitten was Head of Cushions. Now a mat has appeared, and somebody must inspect it.

The cat sniffs one corner, studies another, then settles on a cushion beside it. You have permission to begin. Probably.

There are five movements today. Mitten has brought no whistle, no stopwatch and no qualifications. The cat has brought a very firm opinion about the ownership of soft furnishings.

You do not have to earn anybody's approval. Choose the easier version whenever you need it. Move at your own comfortable pace. The inspector will cope.

And if Mitten loses interest halfway through, that is simply the end of the inspection. You can still finish your session.

### Before you begin

Move in a comfortable range. Use an easier version whenever you need it. Stop if pain, chest pain, severe dizziness or feeling faint develops.

If balance is uncertain, work near a wall or sturdy chair and keep support within easy reach.

The existing easier versions and stop guidance apply throughout. Headstand is outside this standard session.

### 1. Cat Stretch / Cat-Cow

**Movement:** 40 seconds · **Rest afterwards:** 30 seconds.

**Setup narration**

Start by kneeling on your hands and knees. Move gently between a soft arch and a comfortable rounded back. Mitten watches from beside the mat. Apparently the cat would like to know why a human is borrowing the cat stretch.

**Movement cue**

Move slowly. Let the movement follow your breathing. Mitten can wait for the paperwork.

**Easier version**

Keep the movement small if your back feels sensitive.

**Rest scene**

Mitten briefly considers checking your form again. Then a sunbeam reaches the cushion. The inspection department takes a break.

**Next movement**

Next is a short plank. Knees down is absolutely fine.

### 2. Forearm Plank

**Movement:** 30 seconds · **Rest afterwards:** 45 seconds.

**Setup narration**

Set your elbows under your shoulders. Choose toes, knees, or a raised surface. Mitten has settled on a cushion to supervise. The inspector appears to have confused a workout with an invitation to lie down.

**Movement cue**

Stay long through the body. Breathe steadily. Choose the version that feels right for you.

**Easier version**

Shorten the hold or use knees down if your back, neck or shoulders complain.

**Rest scene**

The inspector changes position. This involves turning round twice and lying down exactly where the inspector was before.

**Next movement**

Next is the push-up. Knees down or a higher surface is fine.

### 3. Push-Up

**Movement:** 30 seconds · **Rest afterwards:** 45 seconds.

**Setup narration**

Hands under shoulders. Choose a full push-up, knees down, or a higher surface. Mitten is counting from the cushion. One blink, two blinks, three blinks. None of these counts as a push-up, but the inspector seems satisfied.

**Movement cue**

Lower with control. Clean repetitions, not rushed ones. You set the pace.

**Easier version**

Use a wall or secure countertop if floor push-ups are uncomfortable.

**Rest scene**

Mitten checks the room for a second inspector. There is only a reflection in the window. The department has apparently doubled in size.

**Next movement**

Next we turn onto the side for leg circles.

### 4. Side-Lying Leg Circles

**Movement:** 60 seconds · **Rest afterwards:** 40 seconds.

**Setup narration**

Lie comfortably on your side. We will do thirty seconds on each side. When the countdown says thirty, change sides. Mitten considers these small circles a promising new way of drawing a saucer. A saucer without lunch is less impressive.

**Movement cue**

Small smooth circles. Keep the hips as steady as you comfortably can.

**Easier version**

Bend the moving knee slightly and make a smaller circle if needed.

**Rest scene**

The reflection declines to help with the bridge. Mitten makes a mental note to review staffing.

**Next movement**

Next is the bridge. Mitten has appointed a bridge inspector: Mitten.

### 5. Glute Bridge

**Movement:** 45 seconds · **Rest afterwards:** 45 seconds.

**Setup narration**

Lie on your back with knees bent and feet comfortable. Lift only as high as feels good. Mitten looks up from the cushion. At last, a bridge. The inspector checks whether it leads to the kitchen. It does not.

**Movement cue**

Lift gently, pause, and lower with control. There is no need to impress the inspector.

**Easier version**

Use a mini bridge if your back feels sensitive.

**Rest scene**

All five movements are finished. Mitten closes both eyes. The department is now shut until further notice.

**Next movement**

That completes the standard session. Mitten approves, although the kitchen remains disappointingly far away.

### The inspection is over

Session complete. Get up slowly and notice how you feel.

Mitten stretches on the cushion. The inspection is over. The cat has found the mat satisfactory, the breathing acceptable and the absence of lunch puzzling.

You have given yourself a little time to move. That matters more than a perfect performance.

Mitten offers a purr. There is no certificate, no medal and no ceremony. Just you, a mat, and a cat who has finally stopped supervising.

For the moment.
