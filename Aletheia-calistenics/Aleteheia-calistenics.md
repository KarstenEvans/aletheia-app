# Aletheia Calisthenics Story App

Protocol: https://github.com/KarstenEvans/Aletheia-protocol  
Companion humour protocol: https://github.com/KarstenEvans/thalia-protocol

## Purpose
A mobile-first, free-first story workout player. It turns exercise images into timed narrated scenes, zooms to the current movement, counts down, provides rest periods and rotates short health/healthy-ageing messages.

## First workout
**Calisthenics with Cats**: Cat-Cow, Forearm Plank, Push-Up, Side-Lying Leg Circles and Glute Bridge. The poster's headstand remains an advanced optional demonstration and is deliberately excluded from normal autoplay.

## Audio
The first release uses procedural browser-generated music derived from the earlier Aletheia Frequency Explorer idea. No commercial recording is bundled. Profiles: Zen Garden 60 BPM, Tao Flow 60 BPM and Upbeat 120 BPM.

## Outputs
- mobile/desktop Story App
- static HTM promotional front
- YouTube slide/video export
- social teaser cards
