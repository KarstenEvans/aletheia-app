Why muscle and balance matter

The NHS advises older adults to be active every day and to include activities that improve strength, balance and flexibility on at least two days a week. Bodyweight exercises such as push-ups can count as muscle-strengthening activity, and balance work is particularly relevant for people worried about falling.
