## Recommended gift categories

### 1. Non-slip exercise mat
**Why it fits:** used directly for cat-cow, plank, push-up, leg circles and bridge.  
**What to look for:** good grip, flat edges that do not curl, enough knee/elbow cushioning, easy-clean surface.  
**Awin candidates:** Decathlon UK (MID 26895), Sweatband (MID 2537), Fitness Options (MID 899).

### 2. Yoga blocks / support blocks
**Why it fits:** useful for gentler floor positioning, modified mobility and making some positions more accessible.  
**What to look for:** stable, firm blocks with rounded edges and a non-slip surface.  
**Awin candidates:** Decathlon UK, Sweatband.

### 3. Light resistance bands / mini bands
**Why it fits:** not required for the starter workout but useful for later progression and future Aletheia strength sessions.  
**What to look for:** clearly marked resistance levels, comfortable material, storage bag, no unnecessary complexity.  
**Awin candidates:** Decathlon UK, Sweatband, Fitness Options.

### 4. Foam roller / massage ball
**Why it fits:** optional recovery/mobility gift, especially for users who enjoy post-session self-massage.  
**What to look for:** moderate density rather than aggressive spikes for the general 50+ audience.  
**Awin candidates:** Decathlon UK, Sweatband, Fitness Options.

### 5. Balance pad
**Why it fits:** relevant to future balance-focused sessions, but should be presented as an optional progression tool rather than a default surface for users with poor balance.  
**What to look for:** stable foam, sensible footprint, clearly described use.  
**Safety note:** do not imply that an unstable pad is appropriate for someone already at high fall risk.  
**Awin candidates:** Decathlon UK, Sweatband, Fitness Options.

### 6. Phone / tablet stand
**Why it fits:** genuinely useful for a Story Workout app because the timer, image zoom and captions need to remain visible while the user is on the floor.  
**What to look for:** wide stable base, enough tilt, no need to hold the device.  
**Awin candidates:** Currys (MID 1599); Robert Dyas (MID 1528) may also have suitable home/tech accessories.

### 7. Small Bluetooth speaker
**Why it fits:** improves audibility of trainer narration and generated Zen music without requiring headphones during exercise.  
**What to look for:** simple controls, decent spoken-word clarity, modest size, easy charging.  
**Awin candidates:** Currys (MID 1599).

### 8. Women's activewear
**Why it fits:** the app imagery and first audience skew toward women 50+, so comfortable leggings/tops are a relevant optional gift category.  
**What to look for:** comfortable waist, stretch, coverage, easy movement and straightforward sizing.  
**Awin candidates:** Sweaty Betty UK (MID 32539), lululemon UK (MID 57911), Adanola UK (MID 23775), Vuori UK (MID 41094).

### 9. Trainers / stable exercise footwear
**Why it fits:** useful for standing or chair-assisted sessions rather than mat-only barefoot work.  
**What to look for:** fit, grip and comfort rather than fashion claims.  
**Awin candidates:** Decathlon UK; adidas UK (MID 77022).

### 10. Cat-themed fitness / personalised gifts
**Why it fits:** directly supports the Calisthenics with Cats identity without pretending the cat is equipment.  
**Ideas:** personalised water bottle, cat-themed gym towel, exercise journal, mat bag, mug, T-shirt.  
**Awin candidate:** Not On The High Street (MID 18484), which represents many UK small creative businesses.

## Affiliate implementation rule

Do **not** add a generic merchant homepage and label it as a product recommendation.

For any product finally added:
1. confirm the user's Awin account is accepted into the merchant programme;
2. choose a product that actually fits the stated use;
3. use a product-level/deep-link destination;
4. record product name, merchant, check date and whether price/stock was actually checked;
5. keep the non-affiliate editorial reason visible;
6. disclose affiliate status clearly;
7. keep evidence/safety links outside indiscriminate affiliate conversion.
